## Matrix Rules

- `operation_scope` is the first split field and must stay authoritative:
  - `first_hop`
  - `destination_lifecycle`
  - `intent_only`
- `runtime_real` rows cite exact trigger, endpoint, and artifact seams
- `latent_intent` and `aspirational` rows do not get upgraded to runtime evidence by inference
- surface-specific Arsenal rows may exist, but only as forms of the same underlying Arsenal promotion seam unless code proves a distinct artifact seam
