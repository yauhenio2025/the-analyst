## Source adjudication and practical limits

The [source orientation](fourth_queue_2026_09_06/source_orientation.md), [initial post-score adjudication](fourth_queue_2026_09_06/post_score_adjudication.md), and [repair adjudication](fourth_queue_2026_09_06/repairs/post_score_adjudication.md) distinguish source judgments from mechanical checks and judge opinions.

- **G5:** a selective citation-use register, with distinct Kuhn uses and no invented graph centrality or density. It does not reproduce every bibliographic detail or verify the cited works themselves.
- **A7:** keeps capitalism's attributed values separate from the author's immanent criticism. Hypothetical autonomous society is not an established priority ranking. The closing population-response finding leaves action open; a judge's interpretation of it as an explicit directive is not supported by the actual row.
- **C4:** preserves causal, constitutive, analogical and explicitly noncausal relations. The employment contract is not turned into a technological cause, and financial applications do not become an unsupported closed cycle. Some scope-basis IDs refer to precursor rows, while actual table evidence resolves correctly.
- **C5:** repairs “if” into its stated direction and reserves “only if” for Roth's actual necessary condition. The possible know-how defense is distinguished from Chen's conclusion. Some conditions remain omitted; reported complete scope is not established exhaustive coverage. The actual regression-test row states systematic blockage before challenging a stronger inference to mere absence.
- **S5:** supplies an ordered learning route and corrective passages without invented learner knowledge, durations or success thresholds. Historicism is indirect and the Laudan/Kuhn tension remains qualified. Its 17 useful findings and source-correct tables support inventory release despite the 0.167 Sonnet deficit.
- **R2:** the repair restores the refusal-to-concede inference, occupational and colonial examples, and notes 2/5. It distinguishes that weaker inference from the definitional monism argument and does not confuse selective borrowing with logical contradiction. Actual audience reception and private motives remain unobserved.
- **I4:** repairs the invalid entire findings check and leaves unspecified policy feasibility unknown. Wage/demand coordination occurs within distributive conflict, without a fabricated common goal or free-rider game. The outcome scope remains inconclusive; retained rows and actual response-table meanings are supported.
- **I11:** repairs the PEACE/EMA attribution: PEACE's operation in 2022 is distinct from EMA's reported after-exclusion development. Supplier voting, financing, proposed memoranda, operation and intended ownership transfers remain distinct. **Coverage is selective:** named people and some organizations are omitted, and malformed scope-review fields produce noisy inconclusive metadata. These shortcomings account for Sonnet 7.000 versus original 8.500. The 26 retained inventory findings nevertheless have verified anchors and source-correct table meanings under the owner's inventory standard. No complete-network claim is established by the scope report.

The checked selection omits the Pentagon/Oman–Australia financing and some procurement mentioned elsewhere, and a static PEACE ownership relation sits in the broader holdings table without being asserted as a new transfer. It is a useful selective inventory, not an exhaustive deal census. The replacement original scores 7.833 Sonnet / 6.333 Sol; it is preserved as a control, including status and quotation limitations.

No higher rule demanding a complete local evidence packet or complete scope record was substituted for the owner's release standard. Where a short anaphoric span needs its immediate source context, the source read checks that context. Mechanical walls establish anchor matches, dimensions and IDs; they do not establish entailment, attribution, exhaustive inspection or absence.
