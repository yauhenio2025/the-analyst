# Argument-family comparison: source evidence and independent ratings

The approved USD 16 trial is complete: **24 readings, 48 independent rubric scores, 174 logical calls, USD 6.173507 in saved-response costs**. The required-complete report validates all products with zero errors. No head-to-head was run. Source conclusions were frozen before any score existed.

The proposed Dialectical Structure and Counterfactual Analyzer designs make several useful distinctions more explicit, but neither is ready for production registration. Source reviews find improvements alongside retained errors, new misreadings, incomplete checking and cumbersome scope displays. The controls help distinguish substantive restraint from failures in the structured report. Ratings are secondary evidence for this review; they do not certify the engines.

## What was compared

The approved [protocol](VALIDATION_argument_family_2026-09-05.md) compares 12 original/candidate pairs: six full-paper cases, two same-author corpora, two inventory absence controls and two mixed-scope controls. Each of the 24 actual final readings receives one independent Sonnet assessment and one independent Sol assessment against the same six criteria. Each rating sees the full source and one reading, without the competing reading, source memo or other rater's scores. No routine head-to-head was run.

The treatment changes questions, framing, checking and corpus decomposition together. Original readings use one Sol call; candidates use Sol plus a check for single documents, and Luna extraction, DeepSeek review and Sol synthesis for corpora. These design-informed sources are not held-out generalization evidence. All final readers/synthesizers are Sol; two raters expose disagreement but cannot identify a causal output-family bias in this matrix. Visible receipts and format differences also limit blinding.
