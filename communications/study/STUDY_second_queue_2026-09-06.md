## Release decisions and remaining work

**No second-queue method is released.** All eleven remain registered and excluded from offers, automatic “More” offers, planning and ordinary path resolution. Existing group names are sufficient for eventual placement; no new purpose group was needed. The exclusions now name the observed hold, rather than saying validation is pending. This is the release decision for this frozen configuration, not a claim that the questions or every reading are bad.

### G2 — Concept comparison and trajectories (`compare_concept_trajectories`)

The Castoriadis pair produces useful local/global scope distinctions and correctly avoids treating an earlier text as causally dependent on a later one. Deep has 14 retained rows and 27/27 normalized anchors; checked has 23 and 33/33. Their four/five corpus rows all have both source keys. Yet the final matrices mostly assign a primary concept to each work rather than inventorying aligned concepts in each source. In deep, F11 quotes the wage/hour struggle but omits the consumer-market and expectation consequences claimed in its causal cell. Checked's general non-neutrality cell relies on a local neutrality concession. Method and metaphor cells also require fuller spans. **Missing:** aligned concept-by-work cells, complete causal/method evidence and preserved scope in each cell. Destination if later ready: “Follow the words.”

### G3 — Borrowing and changed use (`concept_appropriation_tracker` v2)

Both modes recognize that the later article supplies no explicit transmission link to the earlier paper and that external fidelity cannot be checked without the borrowed originals. Both preserve money beyond subsistence. Deep nevertheless calls the Weber salvation-sign-to-substance move a later change even though the 2001 paper already makes it explicitly in its Simmel/Marx discussion. This is a false cross-work change despite 28/28 anchors and five valid two-key rows. Checked's 24/24 anchors do not cure an anchor ending at “also” before the monetary transfer, or a Hayek attribution supported only by a Luhmann span. **Missing:** inspect the earlier positive claim before assigning change, complete the transfer predicate and every named attribution. Destination: “Follow the words.”

### G4 — How revisions are presented (`revision_presentation`)

The outputs correctly retain the earlier anti-utopian conclusion and decline to invent an explicit self-revision relation. The checked/deep outputs have 23/23 and 27/27 normalized anchors, with five paired rows each. They still overstate later caution or epistemic/property-rights support as change: Promise already makes the money/religion comparison conditional and develops social-complexity and generalized-property arguments. Several position-map cells cite brief announcements or cut predicates. **Missing:** distinguish a new presentation or emphasis from a new argument, carry the earlier qualifications alongside the later wording, and support each attributed component. Destination: “Follow the words.”

### A4 — Audit a counterfactual (`counterfactual_analyzer` v2)

The revised Harris reading distinguishes theoretical identity from explanatory inadequacy, grants an opponent premise without endorsing it, and keeps an inferential counterexample without demanding if–then syntax. Hegel's positive derivation is recognized rather than declared absent. However Harris misses the particularly useful no-concession inference and the decolonial rhetorical counterexample; the original control notices the former. Hegel retains two invalid spans (layout/bracket/hyphen changes) and cites rejected F5 twice in the final account. Harris's named Thompson resources and power attribution are incompletely quoted. **Missing:** the actual strongest dependency alternatives, complete attributed spans, and reconciliation of the final table after rejection. Destination: “Test a position.”

## Source adjudication of independent judges

All available criterion reasons and decisive-error fields were read. Raw scores remain unchanged. Their claims were checked against the frozen outputs and local sources; the pre-score memos remain unchanged as well.

- **G2:** Both judges credit the scope and uptake distinctions. Sol's claim that review codes are invented because they are absent from the papers is unsupported: archived steps establish their provenance. Its objection to calling Technique an ontology needs qualification; the quoted passage rejects an ontology that removes making/doing, not every inquiry into technique's ontological status. These objections do not displace the concrete concept-cell/causal-span gaps recorded before scores.
- **G3:** Accept Sol's deep Weber objection: the 2001 source already makes the signal-to-substance transformation. Sonnet positively rewards the same alleged divergence; that reason is rejected. The later Luhmann/Hayek combination is distinctive in its presentation, but a source-true Hayek fact still needs a Hayek-bearing span in its own finding. High checked scores do not supply it.
- **G4:** Accept Sonnet's warning about subordinating entrepreneurship to property-rights expansion: 2022 explicitly retains entrepreneurial innovation and imaginations. Treat changed emphasis as an interpretation, not a demonstrated new causal hierarchy. Sol's modest scope-contraction reservation is also relevant. Neither judge's general praise cures the pre-existing earlier support and clipped-cell evidence problem.
- **A4:** Sol correctly identifies the direct 'precisely because' assertion in Hegel F5 as different from an even-if concession, and notices that rejection did not remove its use. Sonnet says the rejection was handled coherently, overlooking the live table citations. Sonnet's old-Hegel objection partly confuses the analyst's assessment of an unsupported necessity claim with a claim that the author himself hedges it; medium confidence is the analyst's, so that alleged attribution error is not accepted without a sentence actually assigning the hedge to Ganzinger. The concern about imposed counterfactual vocabulary is a fair genre criticism. Both Harris controls have genuine identity/adequacy strengths.
- **A5:** Both judges overlook the literal 'respectively' reversal in Elling F4. It lists reflective then critical readings, then says necessity and minimization are rejected respectively; the source corrects the critical reading's necessity and the reflective reading's minimization. This finding-level error survives their high consistency marks. Sol notices Zambrana's continuing-mediation/practical-consequence drift; Sonnet incorrectly treats the ledger correction as propagated throughout. Analyst-supplied dependency tests need labeling, not exclusion merely because the source does not perform its own audit. Capital's apparently unmediated autonomy must preserve 'seemingly'; it does not mean capital lacks real power to escape particular material limits.
- **A6:** Accept Sol's additional Chen objection: dependence on background values is strengthened into preserving background values, while Roth's quoted thesis states dependence and Chen argues the background may itself be defective. Accept the old-Elling lead objection: necessary natural death does not mean its possible form of political stagnation is itself necessary. Both judges credit the revised conditional-necessity distinctions. Their high anchoring scores still miss the Nazism/populism cell's marriage-only finding and do not make a bracket-normalized quotation literal. Completeness is not established by a positive scope label.

Workflow provenance and presentation are separate. The judges received a blinded product without its receipts, but actual scope records remained visible. Their assertion that every such record is fabricated is not evidence of fabrication. Their complaints about repetition, confusing inconclusive/positive labels and failure of the one-page brief are supported by the delivered text. Mechanical evidence-record failure must not be rewritten as semantic silence or an uninspected source. Likewise, a reviewer is allowed to identify a source's unacknowledged inferential gap; using an analytical term absent from the source is not automatically hallucination when clearly presented as the reviewer's judgment.

- **C1:** Credit the useful voice/scope distinctions and accurate conceptual coverage. Reject Sonnet's alleged output mis-citation of Marx 1976:516: that label occurs in the source paper, not in this generated output. The article itself uses inconsistent year/volume references, but the rater locates its allegation in nonexistent output wording. Its observation that F16 ends at 'independent of' matches the genuine pre-score support gap. Sol's synonymy reservation on the original is reasonable: self-valorization is the distilled form of capital's metamorphoses, not simply interchangeable with every description of value in motion. Missing tables in originals are artifact noncompliance, not proof of worse source reasoning.

- **C6:** Accept Sol's Chen objection: the sentence presenting modern marriage as a straightforward pragmatist learning/capacity success converts Chen's critical test case into an application of the position he challenges. Accept Sol's old-AUKUS objection to one-way explanatory direction: the source explicitly says 'vice versa' for the economic/security relation. General anchoring praise misses the within-paper row erroneously assigned to the corpus dimension and the marriage/childhood support mismatch. External analytical labels clearly marked as inference are not, by themselves, false attribution.

- **S3:** Accept Sol's old-Promise objection to 'ontological equivalence': affinity, analogy and shared faith do not establish identity. Sonnet rewards that very escalation and a quasi-deterministic plot, overlooking the source's conditional ending. Both judges recognize the new subsea reading's useful distinction between narrated institutional perspective and testimony; merely flagging rejected F13 does not repair its citations. The old source's policy correction does not establish a satirical register, as Sonnet correctly notes. Tables absent from original controls are an artifact-format deficit; the judges sometimes falsely say the output itself promised those tables, when that language came from the evaluation request. Keep those levels separate.
