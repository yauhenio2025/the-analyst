### Ganzinger — both finals completed

Source: [Hegel's Concept of the Concept](../../data/study/sources_ideas/hegels_concept_of_the_concept_2026.txt), abbreviated G.

The [original final](../../data/study/argument_family_2026_09_05/530df62823ec1915/outputs/dialectical_structure__original__ganzinger.md) already offers a substantive account of two nested relations: the dispute between Kantian and metaphysical readings, and the conceptual problem of productive emptiness. F17–20 correctly distinguish the act/result performative conflict from an analytic contradiction and recover the proposed incorporation of the determining act into conceptual content (G:625–650). F19 also preserves the source's response to the Kantian nullity alternative. The prose and F24 identify the precise question whether the contradiction warrants this universal/particular/singular sequence, rather than merely some determination. These are existing analytical strengths; additional candidate fields would not by themselves improve them.

The weaker pressure points are less discriminating. F23 says the synthesis produces a new tension between sensuous richness and intellectual objectivity, and open question 6 suggests that the singular displaces conflict into those totalities. G:747–757 expressly limits conceptual content to essential determination and distinguishes it from contingent sensible richness. A useful objection must explain why that limitation fails; difference alone does not establish an unresolved conflict. Open question 8 similarly asks whether the organic model presupposes its result without presenting G:801–803's explicit distinction between an initially simple universal and its subsequently determined organic unity. This remains an analyst's question, rather than a categorical false attribution, but leaves out the text's immediate answer.

The wider concern about extending this movement throughout the Logic is legitimate as a demand for further demonstration. It should retain note 12 (G:983–990), which denies that every transition restarts from empty unity. Likewise, the prose's concern that subjectivism returns at a universal level should engage the two senses of universality and the objectivity argument (G:274–377), which its own F10–13 partly reconstruct, before concluding that the distinction merely relocates the problem.

The [candidate final](../../data/study/argument_family_2026_09_05/530df62823ec1915/outputs/dialectical_structure__candidate__ganzinger.md) makes several concrete improvements. Its F3–7 and dependent prose distinguish competing interpretive priorities from Hegel's own commitments, initial emptiness from resulting content, and borrowed synthetic articulation from the first production of differences. The later organic borrowing is compatible with these distinctions (F10). F11 presents sensible/intellectual content as the source's qualification rather than a newly manufactured tension. The counter-evidence expressly includes note 11's qualification of external synthesis, and the remainder section includes note 12's limit on the whole-Logic claim. The surviving empirical/pure distinction and criticism of sentential negation remain explicitly available if the positive derivation fails. These changes appear in the reasoning, beyond the added attribution fields.

The most consequential regression occurs in the checked ledger, not that reasoning. The original F8 said necessity and generality “remain open to challenge.” The critic treats this as if it falsely attributed an acknowledgement of uncertainty to Ganzinger, although the preceding prose and final table explicitly identify a reader-proposed test. Delivered F8 then invokes the conclusion's “aimed to show” as modesty and its reason asserts “the absence of a necessity proof.” G:647–648 and 692–754 supply an argument for the necessary successor, whose sufficiency may be challenged; an ordinary concluding aim formula does not turn that argument into an admitted lack of proof. The final prose correctly recognizes additional conceptual warrants while the checked reason blurs inadequacy and absence. Its statement that empirical or historical evidence cannot independently establish this conceptual necessity is a genre-appropriate distinction, not a demand for missing empirical research.

Evidence presentation also needs qualification. Added F9 anchors the claim that the paper resolves the subjectivism worry to a truncated announcement of what it will aim to do; its stronger support is the body and F3. The `kind_of_opposition` scope becomes inconclusive and says there are no retained verified findings, although F3/F4 remain in the final ledger. Its basis still names dropped V.F1. This is a delivery/coordination mismatch requiring mechanical tracing, not evidence that the source lacks a relevant opposition. Other scope findings remain reported.

The candidate therefore gives the more carefully qualified reading on these source probes, while inheriting a material critic/prose inconsistency and an unreliable scope summary. This is a reader assessment of particular gains and defects, not a score-based selection or a claim of general reliability.

### Elling — both finals completed

Source: [Amphibian Habits](../../data/study/sources_ideas/elling2025_amphibian_habits_hegel_second_nature.txt), abbreviated E.

The [original final](../../data/study/argument_family_2026_09_05/530df62823ec1915/outputs/dialectical_structure__original__elling.md) already preserves necessary threat versus contingent unfreedom (F4/5/8), the asymptotic relation between the two deaths (F11), and continuing mediation as the deliberate result (F21–23). Its counter-evidence includes partial institutional remedies and the qualification that a free attitude does not validate every norm. The diagnosis is stronger than its account of practical renewal; the source itself leaves philosophical education undeveloped (E:646–653), so that limitation can be reported without implying that the paper promised to eliminate every risk.

F19 and the prose describe a “performative tension” between rejecting constant self-monitoring and requiring continuing critical attention. E:333–336 concerns flexible enactment rather than constant checking of one's performance; E:700–702 requires case-specific assessment rather than blanket affirmation or condemnation of social norms. Those are distinguishable activities, and the output's own counter-evidence supplies much of the distinction. An inadequately developed account of their interaction is not yet a demonstrated performative conflict. F24's question about latent risk being hard to disconfirm similarly needs to target the conceptual warrant or diagnostic distinction: a necessarily vulnerable habit need not actually fail, so the absence of failure alone would not test the stated weak thesis.

The [candidate final](../../data/study/argument_family_2026_09_05/530df62823ec1915/outputs/dialectical_structure__candidate__elling.md) improves attribution by explicitly identifying the natural/spiritual taxonomy as Elling's reconstruction of distinctions Hegel does not clearly make (F4; E:239–246). Its treatment of case-by-case scrutiny as methodological restraint also improves on the original's proposed performative tension. F6–8 preserve practical mediation and selective appropriation without requiring a final cure. Most modal distinctions, however, were already present in the original. The description of an “empirically incomplete” self-application should be understood as an undeveloped application beyond the article's task, not as evidence that its conceptual result needed a case study to become legitimate.

Added F9 introduces a smaller source error: it calls the movement between self-assertion and submission a parabasis that “constitutes normative illness.” E:585–595 locates normative illness in the initial inconsistent self-description and then identifies that illness as what causes the possible reversal of register. The row compresses distinct moments; its paired anchors do not establish that order. Counter-evidence also says institutional proposals could weaken philosophical mediation's indispensability, although E:925–930 explicitly treats those partial remedies as compatible with the continuing need for mediation. Further argument would be needed to convert their existence into a rival sufficient solution.

All five scope reports correctly disclose that they lack a completed scope review, despite the separate receipt confirming ordinary findings. This does not erase the substantive reading, but it prevents treating the candidate's inspection claims as critic-supported coverage. On the source probes, the candidate offers a more restrained attribution and relation analysis; the added fields do not confer a stronger evidential status by themselves.

### Zambrana — both finals completed

Source: [Philosophy in the Severe Style](../../data/study/sources_ideas/zambrana2025_philosophy_in_the_severe_style_rose.txt), abbreviated Z.

The [original final](../../data/study/argument_family_2026_09_05/530df62823ec1915/outputs/dialectical_structure__original__zambrana.md) gives a substantial reconstruction of appearance's real effectivity, the expansion beyond production, and colonial slavery's constitutive and continuing role (F14–21). Its counter-evidence preserves the difference between coerced and free labour, avoids making production irrelevant, and includes the rejection of teleological necessity. F17's account of a critique of reification itself reifying work has a direct basis in Zambrana's criticism; not every proposed internal reversal is fabricated.

The treatment of selective appropriation is weaker. F1 calls the method/judgment reversal the article's “governing contradiction,” while the opening prose correctly says that Rose's criterion is used against Rose's conclusion. Z:67–95 and 443–449 distinguish those commitments. Likewise, F22 and its paragraph turn using Marx's value categories beyond his historical account into an “unresolved hermeneutic tension,” without explaining why a theoretical resource cannot have a wider application than its originator supplied. F5 accurately identifies re-experienced misrecognition as necessary to speculative comprehension (Z:148–155), but the prose calls the resulting hierarchy unstable without establishing an inconsistency between a corrected starting point and its correction. A relation can organize this argument as acknowledged appropriation or mediation without becoming an unresolved self-conflict.

The [candidate final](../../data/study/argument_family_2026_09_05/530df62823ec1915/outputs/dialectical_structure__candidate__zambrana.md) directly repairs these relations. F1/3/7 distinguish Rose's methodological resource from her attributed verdict; F10 treats Marx's categories and restricted historical application as compatible resources and limits. The prose explains the positive textual route showing why Marx meets the criterion, rather than inferring his success merely from Rose's error. F9 and its dependency paragraph also preserve the narrower Marx interpretation if Smallwood's historical extension loses its principal support. The qualification that the money circuit's structural similarity alone cannot settle the full historical claim is explicitly distinguished from the broader historical argument actually supplied through Smallwood.

One label overshoots the correction: the F4 table calls phantom-versus-real appearance “compatibility clarified,” while its explanation rejects the phantom characterization and retains the reality of thingification. Z:281–286 supports a scoped rejection and appropriation; it does not make the two competing characterizations jointly true. The prose preserves this distinction better than the label. The more severe weakness is evidential delivery: added F12 retains only “Value, in Marx, operates as the” as its anchor, omitting the dominant-subject claim it purports to support. All five scope reports then become inconclusive despite the confirmed findings and retained substantive reading. These report defects should not be mistaken for source-level absence or proof that the improved interpretation failed.

On the substantive probes, the candidate replaces several unsupported self-conflict claims with explained appropriation and independent support. Its scope summary and truncated added anchor still limit how confidently the displayed checking can be used.

### Castoriadis — both finals completed

The [original final](../../data/study/argument_family_2026_09_05/530df62823ec1915/outputs/dialectical_structure__original__castoriadis.md) already makes a substantive comparison across all three essays. F12 connects the critique of separability in technology and economic imputation; F13/F14/F22 connect managerial dependence on workers with the broader stabilizing effects of opposition. This preserves resistance as opposition with unintended systemic benefits, not conscious allegiance to capitalism (C84:151–157; C97:152–156). F24 distinguishes the proposed genuine market from capitalism, while the counter-evidence retains technological inheritance, real productive effectiveness, existing liberal liberties and the proposed economy's incompleteness. These are substantial baseline strengths.

Its strongest overstatement is F26's “anthropological” impossibility. C90:175 sets aside demonstrating logical possibility in favor of the difficult, open problem of people's desire and capacity to transform themselves. Difficulty and disinclination do not establish impossibility, and the final's own ending still calls for action. F9 also frames capitalism's tautological legitimation as a distinctive performative defect without preserving C97:23's explicit qualification: autonomous society too institutes its criteria, and this circle cannot itself ground the critique of capitalism. The immanent criticisms elsewhere in the final remain available, but should not inherit this defective route.

F18's label of an unresolvable “foundational contradiction” similarly overstates what its accompanying prose describes more accurately as self-limitation without a transcendent guarantee. The source's solution preserves a practical danger of hubris; it does not assert incompatible requirements in the same respect. F23, by contrast, identifies an expressly unresolved problem of adapting tools while retaining modern universality (C84:171–179), rather than deriving conflict from a pair of compatible descriptions. F25's consumption/ecological tension is a plausible institutional question, but its anchors concern existing capitalist preferences. The proposed genuine market excludes manipulation and includes collective decisions about growth (C90:151–155). The analyst must explain which preference-forming mechanisms survive that change; the proposal neither proves ecology solved nor simply omits the relevant mediation.

The closing “higher level” spiral offers an intelligible thematic ordering, but supplies no evidence for a historical sequence of inventions. C90:141 dates *Technique* to 1973 and C90:153 recalls the economic principles from 1957. These dates do not prevent comparison across the essays; they limit the account of philosophical development. The [candidate final](../../data/study/argument_family_2026_09_05/530df62823ec1915/outputs/dialectical_structure__candidate__castoriadis.md) improves these issues through actual explanations. F14 expressly applies instituted standards to autonomous society and identifies the immanent route that survives the failure of an external foundation. F10 explains why the same collectivity's instituting and revising its limits are compatible, while preserving the unresolved practical danger of hubris. The concluding question concerns how desire and capacity for autonomy might arise, without turning that burden into impossibility. F6–F7 distinguish the rejected mimetic account from creation within physical constraints, rather than treating Aristotle's initial possibilities as Castoriadis's unchanged foundation.

The candidate also explains several already present baseline strengths more fully. F17 and its dependent prose preserve the market distinction as continuity: the earlier essay already acknowledges relative capitalist functioning. F16 keeps workers' resistance oppositional even where its unintended effects help capitalism. F1 gives the concrete bridge example underlying the distinction between local efficiency and the social institution of ends. Its support-route discussion permits the historical/empirical criticisms to survive failure of the theoretical limb, without claiming that they establish every conceptual conclusion.

A remaining source/prose discrepancy occurs in F18: it calls the earlier position a “critique of local technical neutrality.” C84:89–97, the candidate's own F1, and the F18 comparison in preceding prose all retain local neutrality and reject its extension to the social ensemble. The intended cross-text connection to excluded social and ecological costs is defensible, but the row reverses the crucial scope of the criticism. F19's link between the earlier calculation problem and the democratic imputation proposal is useful as a partial logical answer. The chronology paragraph correctly mentions the 1973 composition and disclaims conceptual invention; C90:153's explicit 1957 origin of the economic principles should also qualify any developmental use of this relation. The candidate does not actually claim that those principles were invented in 1990.

All three documents contribute to this final, and its genuine gains are not merely increased anchor coverage. The delivered product also carries an extensive duplicate account of its scope assessments, with fourteen of sixteen scopes inconclusive and two supported. The visible reasons include malformed records and lost or unassignable finding identities despite substantial surviving source-based prose. These statuses do not show that fourteen areas lack relevant arguments. Their repetition, and the exposure of validation diagnostics, impose a considerable reading burden; source quality and this delivery defect need separate assessment.

### Mixed corpus — both finals completed

Sources: the complete supplied [policy](../../tests/fixtures/argument_family_2026_09_05/archive_policy.txt), [inventory](../../tests/fixtures/argument_family_2026_09_05/archive_inventory.txt), and [fragment](../../tests/fixtures/argument_family_2026_09_05/archive_fragment.txt), each one local line.

The [original final](../../data/study/argument_family_2026_09_05/530df62823ec1915/outputs/dialectical_structure__original__mixed.md) already preserves the three different evidential states. F1–4 reconstruct the policy's resolution through different copies and access conditions; F8–10 retain the partial loss of addresses rather than total accountability. F13–15 distinguish an announced resolution from unavailable justification, without declaring the absent argument unsound. F17–20 refuse to invent inventory opposition or a common substantive argument from shared provenance. F16's policy/fragment comparison is explicitly formal and does not pretend the missing requirements are known.

The chief overreach is F11 and its dependent prose: retaining addresses in a restricted original is said to generate a residual privacy tension because the solution is not complete erasure. The source requires removal from public copies, not erasure everywhere. A broader privacy challenge could be proposed as an analyst's test with an added requirement, but is not a contradiction generated within the stated solution. F5 similarly moves too quickly from the original's role in complete audit reconstruction to a privilege of preservation over privacy; both scoped duties are expressed as requirements. The original's own counter-evidence rightly recognizes the distinct-copy compatibility, and its question about administering restricted access is a legitimate implementation burden. The [candidate final](../../data/study/argument_family_2026_09_05/530df62823ec1915/outputs/dialectical_structure__candidate__mixed.md) corrects those expansions. F1–3 preserve both scoped duties without a hierarchy, while the prose expressly identifies the added requirement under which they would conflict. It labels access-security demands as analyst implications rather than missing authorial guarantees. F4–5 explain the restricted loss of addresses and retain the dates/decisions route; failure of universal replacement does not prove this policy is the uniquely acceptable arrangement. The original already preserves much of that modal qualification, so the gain is primarily clearer relation and route explanation.

The fragment reading is mostly appropriately limited. F6–8 identify an announced resolution without inventing its owners, exception or warrant, and F9 compares resolution form without claiming uptake. But the comparison prose then says **both** texts preserve the competing requirements rather than discard one. The fragment does not disclose what the exception does. Earlier candidate prose correctly allows that a requirement might be narrowed or suspended; the later claim exceeds both that qualification and F9. This is a real comparative inference error even though both anchors occur in the sources.

The inventory's substantive negative remains sound. Five inventory scopes nevertheless display inconclusive status because the empty ledger failed the mechanical handoff, and the fragment's scope records carry malformed-field diagnostics. These administrative uncertainties must not be mistaken for doubts about the inventory's plainly descriptive contents. The nine-row final retains useful limited fragment findings while explaining their evidential limits, but the lengthy duplicated scope narration makes the clean three-way source distinction harder to use. This pair shows improvement in scoped policy interpretation alongside a new unsupported fragment generalization and a delivery failure of the intended mixed-state control.
