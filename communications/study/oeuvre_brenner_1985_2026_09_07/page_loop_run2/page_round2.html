What it seeds

a way station between the negative argument and a later positive mechanism, unresolved until four later texts [L1.F4]

Read forward, the paper is a way station, not an endpoint. [prospective_reading/F2] traces its reproductive conception of social-property relations into the 1986 chapter's causal chain from property form through reproduction rules to developmental outcome. [prospective_reading/F4] traces its problem of feudal actors transforming their own constitutive relations into the 2001 Low Countries account of unintended aggregate transformation. [prospective_reading/F5] shows the 2006 essay restating that unsolved task as the positive formula: transition as the unintended consequence of feudal reproduction. [prospective_reading/F6] shows the 2007 synthesis generalizing the focal reversal of productive-forces determinism into a multi-level causal chain. Four texts complete what the focal article poses but declines to solve, since it names itself only Part I. [prospective_reading/F16] states this as verdict: a way-station, unresolved until later works supply mechanism and comparative history.
