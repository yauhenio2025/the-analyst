## Decisions

Use **two new engines in the existing analytical citation family**, with the cohort exporter, row/anchor vocabulary, admission wall and plan-led desk. There is no new top-level family. `citation_overlap_map` is one direct comparative read per selected shared person P; `citation_overlap_synthesis` compares those maps across P. The map reads A's and B's actual passages together, with P's cited-work holdings as witnesses and the table/plan as context. It reuses the engagement map's passage moves and stances and cohort provenance without modifying the live engines.

Prefer the dedicated map over two mandatory engagement-map runs plus a comparison pass: the paired research question exposes differing texts and nested attribution while passages remain in context; it avoids paying for two full essays before comparison and lets the critic examine every cross-author claim at once. This is a method choice to validate, not a proven quality advantage. Existing final engagement ledgers may be reused if source snapshot, settings, exclusions, passage coverage and applied-ruling provenance match; the comparison still reads their original witnesses. Cached interpretations are not independent corroboration. Two new engagement runs plus a comparison is a fallback or controlled validation condition, never silently an additional default cost.

The map emits side-specific passage/trajectory ledgers and a distinct shared-person comparison ledger. The exporter puts a side's rows under `<author_uid>__<P_uid>::citation_overlap_map::<row_id>`, preserving actual engine ownership; it does **not** relabel them `citation_engagement_map`. Cross-author rows use `<overlap_uid>__<P_uid>::citation_overlap_map::<row_id>`, in `shared::<overlap_uid>__<P_uid>`, with explicit supporting side-row refs. A row cannot manufacture its own side support. A side projection is a view of one immutable map artifact, not a second run or a new memo to file. The same artifact hash and origin row mapping appear in the receipt. Optional old engagement, fidelity and reception ledgers retain their engine keys and canonical links under the same side pair.

Surface uses existing `oneshot` (preview, uncriticised), standard `oneshot_checked` (Sol read, DeepSeek critic, applied reconciliation), deep `dvs` (Luna dimension/document reads, DeepSeek verification, Sol synthesis). Routes match the released family exactly; actual resolved models and tokens go in receipts. Deep document-scoped O2 reads citing-author texts only; O1/O3/O4/O5 receive appropriate corpus groups, O4 grouped within author. S1 reads one shared-person document; S6 reads metadata only; S2–S5/S7 require the author × person support grid below. Context and P windows cannot unlock corpus gates. The current per-call `max_rows: 20` is a capacity hint, not permission to omit events: Claude must batch by canonical events, reconcile batches and report inspected/eligible counts before labelling an inventory complete.

## Transport and evidence identities

Stacks sends an approved **overlap table**, a plan and map job IDs (plus optional side-pair job IDs) using its `pair_payload()` boundary. The Mastermind resolves its own stored artifacts into the packet; Stacks is not asked to fabricate normalized ledgers. Use `role: overlap_table`, `key: context:overlap` and `role: plan`, `key: context:plan`. SourceRole patch admits `cohort_table` (Stacks wire spelling) and `overlap_table`, preserving all six roles now on main, including `cohort` (the prior design's normalized spelling). These envelope roles differ from body `SOURCE ROLE: citing_author|primary_window|secondary_reader`. Adding literals alone does not route, profile, validate or make metadata citable.

The new two-author index keeps `role: evidence_index`, `version: overlap-index/v1`, `person`, `texts`, `checks`, `unchecked`, `readers`, `settings` and `plan`. Replace singular `author` with exactly two `authors: [{uid,id,name}, {uid,id,name}]` (id is the optional Stacks slug; uid is the resolved person identity). Every `texts[]` has `author: <uid>` and original `uid` and/or `key`, title, year or null, creators, passages, and an explicit `coauthored_with: []` or UIDs. Author identity is not a free-text creator string. No singular/plural mixture, repeated author, unknown author, or author inferred from array position. UIDs cannot contain whitespace, `__` or `::`; the author and subject are distinct. Index and table author order must match; flipping display order yields a new revision but never changes the identity behind a UID.

Each passage retains integer/string `ref_id` as supplied and canonical `event_id` generated by the Stacks' ledger (namespace `<author_uid>::<canonical-local-event>`). Distinct copies/editions stay distinct. `unchecked[]` is side-qualified as well. Unresolved/unheld checks remain explicitly unchecked.

`roles` is the existing original-key → role-string map, required and authoritative for all supplied texts. Match UID, Zotero key and explicit ZOTERO UID header aliases before assigning role and `CITING AUTHOR UID` headers. Keep original physical document keys; do not manufacture `A::doc` and `B::doc` aliases of one source. Reject conflicting aliases, duplicate physical witnesses, header/role mismatches, unclassified supplied sources, cross-side reuse of a citing text, and A+B coauthored documents in the analytical index. Other coauthors stay in attribution metadata. Role-map entries for withheld readers are permitted metadata; readers are filtered out of the overlap map, while held primary windows are available for identity/context, not new fidelity verdicts.

The patch preserves the one-author entrypoint unchanged through a separate validated overlap path. An overlap index is accepted only by `citation_overlap_map`: passing it unsliced to an engagement/fidelity/reception engine refuses, avoiding a silent pooled author. Claude's optional audit adapter slices texts/checks/unchecked by author, converts authors→author, retains the canonical event namespace and supplies only that author's roles plus P witnesses. It then runs the existing fidelity engine. The old memo-statements mode remains unchanged. The patch supplies a tested slicing helper; runtime scheduling remains Claude's work.

`locus.how=none` is unlocated and has no fabricated page. Keep `printed`, `pdf`, `epub`, cited pages, copy edition, language, registry work/edition/section IDs, `section_title`, `copy.how`, and retrieval `windows[].how=page|section|search`. A work-family match does not prove page equivalence. Prefer the exact marked rendition used by the job. Window text is a witness, not the model's reconstruction of P.

## The packet: an explicit extension, without breaking cohort-packet/v1

Choose **`overlap-packet/v1` as a versioned extension of the cohort contract**, sharing a single adapter/validator library and the same row/anchor/artifact primitives. Do not add an optional `mode` to the strict v1 cohort object: that schema has `additionalProperties: false` and singular author/engagement requirements. The provided standalone schema is self-contained, with cohort row and anchor definitions reused by the builder. A future common union schema can discriminate on `schema_version`; existing cohort packets and tests stay byte-compatible. A direct overlap-map side projection is admissible only in the overlap schema; sending it as a cohort engagement ledger must fail until a separate explicit cohort revision permits it.

| Cohort v1 field/rule | Overlap extension |
|---|---|
| `author` | `authors` is an ordered array of exactly two distinct resolved identities; semantic keys never depend on A/B position. |
| `cohort` | `overlap_table` has approved shared selection, complete side counts, thresholds, identity rules, residues, collective blocks and coverage. |
| `pairs` | Two side pairs per selected P: `<A_uid>__<P_uid>` and `<B_uid>__<P_uid>`. Each is a completed direct map side projection or compatible completed engagement ledger, with optional audits. |
| one `pair::` document per P | Two side documents plus one `shared::<overlap_uid>__<P_uid>` comparison document per P; all engines for one side remain one pair document. Extra documents cannot inflate person count. |
| required engagement ledger | Direct side projection has owning engine `citation_overlap_map`; cached side analysis can instead use `citation_engagement_map`. An explicit reviewed empty side ledger is accepted as no retained interpretation, not as support for a comparison. |
| immutable row ref and source sidecar | Same row fields, raw artifact hash, applied status, anchor status, canonical_ref, original anchors/loci. Shared rows additionally name every supporting side-row ref in `fields.support_refs`. |
| `not_engaged_residue` | Required `residues` blocks for each author, with all/top_n/unknown completeness. R_A is A-positive/B-zero, R_B vice versa, under the same frozen coverage and settings. |
| collective mentions | Required `collective` blocks per side (explicit empty allowed), separate term/event identities and optional future collective-analysis refs; never allocate them to persons. |
| counts per member | Canonical events, text/year/kind and works per person per author, both entire-universe denominators and shared-person counts. |
| synthesis member gate | Cross-person findings require two or more shared P and BOTH authors for every compared P. A before/after convergence claim additionally requires two dated text endpoints for every author–P cell. |

`fixtures/overlap_packet.schema.json` defines the envelope: schema_version, packet_id/revision, fixture_only, authors, overlap_table, plan, pairs, shared_maps, source_documents. `pairs` keep cohort engine-labelled artifacts and exact original source anchors; `shared_maps` hold comparison artifacts and support refs. `source_documents` adds author_uid (null for P), role, year, coauthors and fixture_only to each hashed rendition. Row status and anchor status are applied-export facts; a substring/hash check cannot prove the critic actually confirmed the row. Keep the cohort's companion receipt contract: raw critic/reconciliation/wall artifacts, row offsets, rename/merge lineage, per-cell refs, engine/depth/models, source blob/locus mapping, memo canonical URI and receipt costs, with original and exported hashes. Missing raw provenance is a pending export, never a memo-to-ledger reconstruction.

The table is a strict normalized analytical snapshot distinct from Stacks' UI response. `rows` contains the complete union of known person identities (shared, exclusive, below threshold and unknown) with `by_author` counts, canonical events, text/year/kind, cited works, registry/edition/held-copy metadata; `selected` is the approved K subset of threshold-eligible shared UIDs. The owner may strike/add among eligible resolved persons; an added below-threshold person requires an explicit revised threshold/scope, never silent promotion. Shared threshold default is ≥1 event per side. Select top-K by descending min(events_A,events_B), then sum, then UID for a deterministic tie; owner changes recorded with reasons. Selection is not an intellectual ranking.

`coverage[uid]` records held/inspected/missing text keys, source snapshot, aliases resolved, ledger_complete, settings and unknown years. A negative count is invalid; unknown is null. Every known count reconciles to unique events and by-text/year/kind rollups; canonical events may occur under several persons, so whole-universe counts use a union. `works[]` records resolved cited key, registry work/edition IDs or null, title as cited, events, copies with key/edition/how and held/fetched/wanted state. Holdings are distinct from citations. Calculate exact-key, registry-work and edition intersections only for resolved entries; preserve unresolved denominators. Each metrics block carries `complete`: false when any count/identity coverage or selected work identity is unresolved; its intersection/union then describes resolved known entries only, never the complete universe. Report Jaccard as |intersection|/|union| or null for empty union, labelled by that completeness. Never average per-person Jaccards into the universe metric.

`residues[uid]` carries `person_uids`, `total`, completeness, top_n or null and omitted count; top_n uses own events descending then UID. Default request is **all** for reasoning, with top 15 as presentation. Below-threshold positive persons are not residues. Unknown identity/count rows are not zeros. Separate `below_threshold` and `unselected_shared` are derived from complete rows, and the harness reconciles them. If Stacks supplies only a truncated outer fan, label the full-union table incomplete, disallow exhaustive zero/overlap arithmetic and retain only a partial metadata report until the complete union/coverage arrives.

`collective[uid]` has terms, canonical events, source keys/loci and metadata row anchors, plus analysis_status not_run|run|unavailable. V1 supports metadata only; status run requires a future validated collective adapter and cannot invent a school reading here. `excluded_coauthored` preserves A+B text UIDs/keys/creators and event totals in an annex, never a third author in shared tests. Optional future third-column display is distinct metadata with its own denominator; it never unlocks overlap or convergence.

## Admission, rendering and output validation

Before any paid call, reject unapproved/incomplete selected jobs, missing author pairs/maps, identity/alias collisions, stale artifact/source digests, wrong namespaces, omitted residue/collective blocks, nonreconciling counts, false zeros or unresolved source attribution. A reviewed empty ledger is allowed but contributes no warrant. Missing optional audits are allowed with explicit limits. Metadata and plan documents are rendered separately; context:overlap is admitted to walls solely for table facts, context:plan never to evidence. Direct map bodies are the original author passages and P windows; synthesis documents render one physical line per row with full literal anchors and engine/ref/hash headings. Side documents preserve original scholar identities even though the synthesis wall also checks the carried text in the rendered row.

A required structured sidecar accompanies each final finding: id, dimension, kind (`side_report`, `single_person`, `cross_person`, `period`, `fidelity`, `metadata`), person_uids, author_uids, row_refs, inherited anchors, metadata_refs, and period endpoints where needed. Every substantive table cell has finding_ids or metadata_refs, reconciled to the final retained output. All inherited anchors must survive, including both A-or-B and P anchors of an audit. Normalize only serialization escaping; raw text is not corrected. Aliases link canonical rows but cannot be cited as extra support. A passage quoted under the wrong document/author fails even if the words appear elsewhere.

The offline harness checks hash/shape/key joins, canonical event arithmetic, threshold and residue reconciliation, side ancestry, source re-finding, required author×person grid, dated endpoints, fixture flags and cell references. It is an executable design prototype, not the production exporter or a semantic validator. It does not infer actual voice, evaluate a reliance ordering, prove a critic ruling from normalized flags, or verify that an entire source was semantically read. Claude must add runtime integration tests for key preservation and metadata routing through every read, critic, reconcile, wall, desk and compose stage; no generic two-document check can replace the author×person grid.

Zero selected shared persons yields metadata and residues only; one yields a one-person comparison plus scope, never a shared-canon pattern. Failed or missing required jobs cannot be silently omitted from selected K; the owner can explicitly revise selection. A mixed real/synthetic packet remains fixture_only and is rejected by a release check.
