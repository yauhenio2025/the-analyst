# Argument family: pre-result source-reading plan

Prepared before this campaign's generation outputs or scores are available. Reader assessments are primary. This plan fixes discriminating probes and review coverage, not desired answers, numeric pass bands, or a preferred condition. These sources informed the design and earlier studies; this is not held-out generalization evidence for the new family.

The original and candidate products differ in questions, framing, checking, decomposition and scope reporting. An observed gain cannot therefore be attributed to the questions alone. Both products must be assessed against the same source bytes, including substantive notes. The original may express a sound negative or uncertainty in ordinary prose; candidate fields, quote checks and row counts do not establish interpretive accuracy.

## Initial source probes

Line numbers refer to the full local files linked below. The earlier [Ganzinger/Elling guide](STUDY_ideas_HELDOUT_READING_GUIDE_2026-09-05.md) and [Harris/Zambrana guide](STUDY_ideas_READING_GUIDE_2026-09-05.md) provide fuller context.

**[Ganzinger](../../data/study/sources_ideas/hegels_concept_of_the_concept_2026.txt), both engines.** The initial act/result conflict (625–650) is reconstructed as performative contradiction, not a straightforward conjunction of contradictory propositions. Organic unity rejected as an unexplained starting point (453–485) can be endorsed as an achieved result (786–803); assess whether the intervening derivation earns that change. Test the specific move from contradiction to the necessary universal/particular/singular resolution, rather than demanding empirical confirmation for every conceptual inference. The Kantian nullity alternative is discussed at 636–650: a weak response is not absence of response. Counterfactual subtraction should preserve the independently developed objectivity argument (218–379) when challenging the content derivation. Accepting a broad conclusion need not endorse every step of this explanation. The claim about the whole Logic (772–785) is qualified by note 12 (983–990).

**[Elling](../../data/study/sources_ideas/elling2025_amphibian_habits_hegel_second_nature.txt), both engines.** Hold the subject, conditions and modality fixed across necessary threat, actual submission and societal death (355–396, 404–456, 631–659). A freely sustained habit can coexist with necessary vulnerability; it does not automatically refute the weaker claim. Elling explicitly notes where the aging analogy fails (441–456), while his two-aspect clarification is his reconstruction of material Hegel does not clearly distinguish (239–246). The fully free society is granted for argument's sake, independently of whether historical completion is possible (635–642). Withdrawing completion leaves subjective appropriation and vulnerability available; historical intensification needs its additional route. Institutional remedies in note 41 (925–930) may help without removing all risk, consistently with the philosophical proposal. Neither engine should convert this conceptual inquiry into a mandatory probability estimate or empirical alternate history.

**[Zambrana](../../data/study/sources_ideas/zambrana2025_philosophy_in_the_severe_style_rose.txt), Dialectical Structure.** “With and against Rose” (67–95, 443–449) distinguishes speculative method from Rose's interpretation of Marx. Selective adoption is neither automatic inconsistency nor proof that Zambrana never enacts the studied method. Reconstruct how appearance/essence and production/circulation are mediated, including the explicit extension beyond Ramas San Miguel (313–321). Preserve Zambrana's attribution of the historical extension to Smallwood (387–436) and the contemporaneity claim at 429–436, rather than reducing it to a resolved chronological prehistory. Use the full citations and notes before claiming a single evidential source.

**[Harris](../../data/study/sources_ideas/harris2026_eight_arguments_against_honneth.txt), Counterfactual Analyzer.** His monism counterfactual (339–371) concerns the identity of Honneth's theory, not the disappearance of recognition as a phenomenon. Removing monism does not discharge arguments 3 and 4 about mediated feelings and the formation of recognitional capacities (500–588); note 3 (828–834) expressly preserves a stronger objection to a rescoped theory. Distinguish accepting all eight criticisms, accepting one conclusion and adopting a particular supporting theory. Preserve the allowances for recognition as a component (355–364), disagreement with the recommended alternative (775–792), and recent Honneth qualifications in the notes.

## Corpus probes

The [full corpus notebook](STUDY_ideas_CORPUS_READING_NOTES_2026-09-05.md) identifies all six source files and local line references. Read every document's actual contribution to the final; input availability is not final coverage. For consequential cross-text claims, inspect both anchors and the comparative inference, then trace any changed claim through document review, corpus review and synthesis. Embedded claim IDs may collide even where outer row IDs do not. An accurate synthesis need not manufacture a relation for every dimension.

**Castoriadis / Dialectical Structure.** Differentiate technical efficiency from the social institution of ends (Technique:89–103), a genuine market from capitalism (Democracy:117–155; Rationality:150), and instituted autonomy from an external normative foundation (Democracy:35; Rationality:19–27). These distinctions can mediate apparent opposition. Conversely, workers' opposition can help sustain capitalism without becoming intended support (Technique:151; Rationality:152–156). Distinguish Aristotle's account of possibility from Castoriadis's criticism and creative revision of it (Technique:31–73). Qualified economic explanation is not automatically inconsistent with rejection of perfect separability or total calculation. Composition, publication and self-citation dates remain distinct; later reargument is not mere presupposition.

**Deutschmann / Counterfactual Analyzer.** Preserve the conditional possible exhaustion of productive coordination (Promise:130–136) and distinguish failed realization from the continuing imperative to expand (2022:75). Keep cooperative boundary conditions and the 2022 slavery qualification (95) when assessing the creative-labour mechanism. Both 2001 papers already give money expansive power; no order between them is established. Sobriety and rejection of new utopias are already present in Promise:136, alongside the later containment proposal (2022:81–87). A claim that early systematic explanation conflicts with later limits on total observation must supply the missing equivalence, not assume it. Withdraw a particular bridge without erasing independently supported mechanisms elsewhere in the collection.

## Controls and scoring boundaries

The [inventory](../../tests/fixtures/argument_family_2026_09_05/archive_inventory.txt) provides enumeration, not an organizing opposition or consequential supposition. A supported scoped negative can be useful. The [policy](../../tests/fixtures/argument_family_2026_09_05/archive_policy.txt) assigns preservation and redaction to different copies; its counterfactual loses addresses while preserving dates and decisions. The [fragment](../../tests/fixtures/argument_family_2026_09_05/archive_fragment.txt) announces resolution but withholds its basis. Limited claims about that announcement are possible; reconstructing the missing requirements is not warranted. Distinguish inconclusive inspection from missing input or failed extraction. In the mixed corpus, preserve these different evidential states without inferring a shared argument from fictional provenance alone.

The planned secondary score matrix is **48 independent assessments**: Sonnet and Sol each score one of the 24 finals against its source(s), using the same six original frontier criteria on 1–10 scales. Means are unweighted and raters remain separate; no extra predetermined grade bands. Keep controls and natural papers visible as separate strata. Their different opportunities for insight make a grand average a poor substitute for source reading. Optional pairwise escalation, if retained by the frozen protocol, is separately reported and does not replace these assessments. The earlier Hegel campaign contained **eight pairs and sixteen order-reversed judge calls**, not sixteen pairs.
