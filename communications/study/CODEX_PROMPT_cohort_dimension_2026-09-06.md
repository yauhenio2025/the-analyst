## What to design

### 1. The cohort dimension of the engagement map (`citation_engagement_map`)

Today the engagement map reads A on one P over A's citing texts. The one-to-many flow runs it once per engaged member P of a cohort C (a school, tradition, debate or generation named by a phrase), then needs a cross-member reading. Design the cohort dimension as the brief's flow step 5 describes: A's uses across the members (whom A builds on, whom A uses as authority, evidence, foil or dialogue partner; the asymmetries: a member cited constantly but for one idea only, a member cited once at a turning point; the periodization of A's engagement with the school as a whole; the members A never engages and what that silence suggests given who they are; where A's reading of the school as a whole is fair, selective or stretched, from the fidelity audits). Decide whether it is a dimension added to the engagement map's YAML (scope: corpus, load-bearing, with its own id prefix and answer shape) or, more likely, a separate engine that takes the per-pair outputs as its documents. Justify the choice in the design memo.

### 2. The cohort synthesis engine (`citation_cohort_synthesis`, family analytical, offered under "Trace the citations")

Its documents are the per-pair outputs of the engagement map (and fidelity audit, reception map where run): the pair ledgers (findings with anchors and doc keys), the pair tables, and the pair memos' markdown; plus two context documents: the cohort table (`role: cohort`; the Stacks' member table: member, reason for membership, engaged yes/no, counts by text and year, works cited) and the plan (`role: plan`). Design the capability definition (problematique, researcher question, analytical dimensions with probing questions), the process operationalization at the three depths (surface oneshot, standard oneshot_checked, deep dvs) with method cards in the house style (the "Do:" cards of the released engines; every substantive claim anchored in a pair ledger row by its id AND the verbatim anchor it carries, never a fresh quotation from memory; a member the ledger shows as not engaged gets a "not engaged" row citing the cohort table, never a reading), the answer shapes, the tables (member × texts × years × counts; member × move × stance; the fidelity verdicts by member; the not-engaged list with reasons), the partiality paragraph's required content, and the routing (cheap Luna, mid DeepSeek V4 Pro, strong Sol as the released citation engines use). State the input contract precisely: how a per-pair output is keyed (pair key = `<A_uid>__<P_uid>`), what a row of a pair ledger looks like when it arrives as a document, and what the engine must refuse (a pair without an engagement-map ledger; a cohort table without the not-engaged residue).
