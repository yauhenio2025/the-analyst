## Decision and analytical unit

Implement `citation_cohort_synthesis` as a separate analytical engine, offered under **Trace the citations only after its live release gate**. Flow step 5's “cohort dimension” is its cross-member reading, not an additional dimension running over A's original texts inside `citation_engagement_map`. The latter keeps responsibility for A → P. A cohort job consumes the completed pair analyses and the approved membership ledger; it can be re-run when one pair or a fetched work changes without re-reading every member. This supersedes the task note's provisional wording that the dimension would be added to the engagement map.

Adding a corpus dimension to the existing map would conflate two corpora (A's texts versus A→P analyses), two kinds of identity (text versus pair), and two evidence layers (primary words versus their verified ledger provenance). It would also invite treating another pair memo as a new witness and changing the live pilot's behavior. A separate engine gives cross-member claims their own `C3`–`C7` prefixes, corpus scope and explicit pair endpoints. `C1` preserves member-level warrant; `C2` handles cohort metadata. Load-bearing C3–C6 retain the central questions even in a preview; C7 is optional reception. Lack of an audit yields an explicit limit, never a fabricated school verdict.

This is an intellectual comparison, not a count ranking. Twelve references can repeat one borrowed distinction; one citation can matter at an argumentative turn. Counts establish frequency. Pair readings establish what the citations accomplish. The model must argue any ordering of reliance, propose defensible periods, and judge the significance of selection. Code decides identity, linkage, admissibility, exact anchors and arithmetic.

## Input contract: cohort-packet/v1

The Stacks sends **the approved cohort table, the plan, and the pair job IDs only**. The Mastermind assembles the immutable revision below from its own job store: applied ledgers and wall/critic rulings, desk tables, memo markdown, receipts and exact source documents. It must not ask the Stacks to manufacture normalized pair exports. The JSON schema and small synthetic packet in `fixtures/` are executable examples of the engine packet; the harness patch enforces invariants that JSON Schema alone cannot express. The job-store adapter specified below is still to be implemented.

- `schema_version: cohort-packet/v1`, `packet_id`, `revision`, `fixture_only`, `author: {uid, name}`, `cohort: {uid, phrase, approved, members, not_engaged_residue, coverage, collective_mentions}`, `plan`, `pairs`, and `source_documents`.
- `author.uid` and each member UID are supplied person keys, never inferred names or work keys. UID strings may contain a library prefix such as `em:` but must not contain `__`, `::` or whitespace, which are reserved delimiters. No silent person merge. Supplied aliases and identity resolution are retained in coverage. The actual Riley and Weber person UIDs must come from the Stacks; a work key is not a substitute.
- Pair key is exactly `<A_uid>__<P_uid>`. One source document per pair, `doc_key: pair::<pair_key>`. It contains engine-labelled final ledgers, tables, memo markdown and links to their canonical artifacts. Multiple engines for a pair remain **one pair document**. Row identity is `<pair_key>::<engine_key>::<row_id>`; local `F1` or `D3.F2` collisions across pairs and engines are expected and safe. Packet revision plus artifact hash makes this immutable. Re-runs create new packet revisions, never silently overwrite a row's meaning.
- Every engaged member has one pair with a `citation_engagement_map` final ledger (including an explicitly reviewed empty ledger when no admissible interpretation survived). Fidelity and reception ledgers are optional with `lens_status` declaring `run`, `not_run` or `unavailable`. Partial/failed pairs remain pending and refuse a claimed completed-cohort submission. The owner can explicitly revise the approved scope; the engine cannot silently drop a member to proceed.
- A ledger contains `engine_key`, `artifact_id`, `artifact_sha256`, `rows`, `reviewed_empty`, and `empty_reason` when empty. Raw artifact bytes are included as `artifact_text`; the digest is checked. `rows` are a machine export of the **applied** ledger, preserving wall status and critic ruling, not rows reconstructed from a memo. An adapter must compare the export to the raw artifact and record the mapping; the harness checks every `raw_row` occurs verbatim in that artifact. Duplicate/alias rows link `canonical_ref`; only the canonical row is counted or cited.
- A row carries `row_id`, `ref`, `dimension`, `claim`, `raw_row`, `status` (`confirmed`, `rejected`, `unresolved`, `superseded`), `anchor_status` (`verified`, `failed`, `unverifiable`), `canonical_ref` (null for canonical), `event_ids`, `anchors`, and engine-specific `fields`. An anchor is `{text, source_doc_key, locus, voice}`. `text` is the exact carried quotation; `locus` preserves printed page, PDF page, section UID/title, edition and `how` where supplied. No OCR correction, stitched snippets or remembered quote. All inherited anchors, including A and P witnesses for a fidelity verdict, must remain in the row; additional anchors cannot be discarded to make it pass.
- `source_documents` is a hash-checked map `{original_key: {text, sha256}}` for the **supplied pair source renditions**, including original A texts, primary windows, or reception readers used by retained rows. These witnesses are for the offline source check and scholar's drill-through. They are not fresh analysis documents for the cohort model. In addition to re-finding an inherited anchor in its rendered pair row, the source-read harness re-finds it in the claimed original rendition. If only pair outputs are sent to the model, the Mastermind must retain this sidecar from its stored source blobs for validation. A pair memo alone cannot satisfy the contract.
- Tables are `{table_key, markdown, row_refs}`; `memo: {markdown, canonical_uri}` is a view of the ledger, not a second witness. Every referenced row must exist and be citable; stale rejected citations fail admission until the export is reconciled. A restatement is linked, never filed twice. Existing memo links are retained, never re-filed under a second person or independently counted.
- The two context documents have `role: cohort`, key `context:cohort`, and `role: plan`, key `context:plan`. Plan contains `sections: [{id, title, questions}]`, `themes: [{key, label, origin}]`, and `warnings`. It orders inquiry and warns about namesakes, nested attribution, editions, translations, school/person distinctions and coauthorship; it cannot establish a historical claim.
- Each cohort member has `uid`, `name`, `membership_role`, `membership_reason`, `membership_source`, `engaged` (`yes`, `no`, `unknown`), `counts` (`total`, `mentions`, `bibliography_only`, `by_text`), `works_cited`, `row_id`, and `anchor`. `by_text` rows give A text key, year or null, count and disjoint canonical event IDs; the full event universe (not selected findings) controls totals. A single event may name two members and count once for each, so a school-wide total uses the union, not the sum. Multiple renditions of one citation share an event ID. A name mention establishes ledger engagement even when its interpretation is mere courtesy or bibliography-only.
- `not_engaged_residue` is a required explicit array of the UIDs of **all** approved members whose checked count is zero and `engaged: no`; an explicit empty array is valid. Every zero needs a complete checked coverage snapshot and resolved identity/aliases. `unknown` counts are null, not zero. Coverage identifies held, inspected and missing A texts, date range, ledger snapshot, event-count unit, search completeness, alias resolution, missing years and coauthorship. Membership reasons have their supplied source or the label `plan_proposal`; hypotheses about silence inherit that uncertainty.
- Collective-school citations remain `collective_mentions` with their own event IDs, school UID and source anchors. They are never allocated to people or counted as a second member. A substantive reading of a collective address needs a separately supplied, verified engagement-map ledger for that school identity, marked `subject_kind: collective`, under `collective_analysis` (future adapter extension). In v1 the engine reports collective mention metadata and states that the collective address has not been analysed. It must not generate the missing reading from the plan or cohort phrase.

A single arriving pair row, illustrative and **fixture-only**, is:

```json
{
  "row_id": "D3.F1",
  "ref": "fixture:A__fixture:P::citation_engagement_map::D3.F1",
  "dimension": "citation_move",
  "claim": "A uses P's distinction as an explicit premise in the fixture argument.",
  "raw_row": "[D3.F1] A uses P's distinction — dim: citation_move — anchor: \"I adopt P's distinction, but only for this example.\" — doc: fixture:text1",
  "status": "confirmed",
  "anchor_status": "verified",
  "canonical_ref": null,
  "event_ids": ["fixture:event1"],
  "anchors": [{"text": "I adopt P's distinction, but only for this example.", "source_doc_key": "fixture:text1", "voice": "A", "locus": {"printed_page": "1", "pdf_page": 2, "section_uid": null, "section_title": null, "edition": "fixture", "how": "page"}}],
  "fields": {"move": "adopted_framework", "stance": "adopts", "year": 2000, "text_key": "fixture:text1"}
}
```

The model sees a human-readable pair document: identity header; each engine's artifact ID/hash; one physical line per final row with its immutable ref, literal `raw_row`, fields and separately rendered carried anchor spans; then navigation-only tables and memo. The original `source_doc_key` travels with each span. JSON escaping is undone when rendering; the wall checks the actual carried text. Pair-row refs do not rename original scholar/source identities. Metadata context is rendered separately with row ID and exact `anchor` text. Stable machine fields travel beside markdown; free text is never parsed to infer a person, verdict or event count.

### Admission, exclusions and degenerate cohorts

Refuse a packet before spending if an engaged member lacks an engagement-map ledger; the residue field is absent or differs from the approved zero rows; person, pair, row or document keys collide; author IDs differ; counts conflict with canonical events; a memo/table points at a rejected or missing row; a claimed verified anchor or raw artifact fails; approval is absent; or a nonempty residue has incomplete ledger/alias coverage. Give concrete missing keys and reasons. Unknown/unresolved members are retained explicitly without fabricated analyses. Missing optional audits/reception are accepted with limits.

One valid engaged pair plus any residue can produce a **limited one-pair coverage report**, not a school-level finding. Zero engaged pairs produce only metadata and the residue. Two renditions or two engines of one pair cannot unlock corpus dimensions. Synthetic fixtures always carry `fixture_only: true`; both any generator and the release checker reject them as release evidence. A fabricated second member is named `Fixture comparator`, never passed off as Lachmann or an historical school member.

**Ownership and entry.** Accept `{cohort, plan, pair_job_ids}` from the Stacks, using the approved member/person identities and author identity in those records and the referenced jobs. ### Source read — all retained findings, not a sample

For every cohort finding, open every cited immutable pair row and the supplied pair document, re-find each carried anchor by code, then open its original source rendition and read surrounding text. Check actor/predicate/object/qualification, nested voice, coauthorship, dates, and the claim's scope. For C3–C5 read all endpoints and the strongest counterexample. For C6 read A and P witness clauses for every cited verdict, preserve edition and `how`, and distinguish selective reading from a global characterization of the school. Read all final table cells, not just their IDs. For C2 inspect the actual member row, membership basis, alias-resolution snapshot, checked corpus and event-count zero; no imaginary reading of a non-engaged member. Hypotheses about silence must remain hypotheses.

Record each finding and cell as releaseable, narrow, reject or unresolved with an explicit reason. No fabrication, attribution reversal, unsupported broadening, rejected row or unverifiable quotation may remain in the **retained** output. Failed raw rows are archived and reported as rejected coverage, not quietly erased from the run history. Re-run affected conditions after a method repair; do not hand-edit a paid output and call it a clean run. Exact anchor verification is a necessary condition, not a semantic pass. Compare final row meanings as well as IDs after renumbering.

### Sonnet rubric and order control

There is no historical cohort-engine original. The preregistered comparison is the **same real input snapshot and plan** sent to this engine at standard depth and to the Stacks' existing essay-over-tables desk. It is a comparator, not retroactively an original. Present both anonymous outputs in A/B then B/A order in independent Sonnet calls (`claude-sonnet-4-6` if still the configured model; freeze the actual model). Each call receives the same complete evidence packet, source-read availability and rubric; hide provider, method, price and previous scores. Rate both outputs independently from 0–10 on six axes and cite offending/supporting row IDs in reasons:

1. Cross-member explanatory reading: argued reliance/foil/dialogue distinctions, not a count recitation.
2. Evidence and attribution: exact pair-row ancestry, retained scholar wording and qualifications, no voice reversal or invented source claim.
3. Asymmetry and periodization: frequency versus consequence distinguished, comparable dated endpoints, composition/coverage alternatives considered.
4. Selection and fidelity: scoped residue, defensible membership uncertainty, supplied audit verdicts and missing witnesses correctly bounded.
5. Tables and coverage: complete approved membership and residue, canonical event denominators, every substantive cell source-correct, no rejected row.
6. Scholarly usefulness and partiality: follows the plan, opens the right passages, keeps pair memo links and clearly says what is held, fetched, missing and unsettled.

Common scale: 0–2 materially unusable or fabricated; 3–4 major omissions/overreach; 5–6 useful inventory with significant limits; 7–8 supported comparative reading with stated limits; 9–10 unusually incisive, complete and source-correct. Record mean, each axis, unsupported claims, missing questions, preferred output or tie, and whether a hard source defect exists. Require the cohort candidate to score at least 7 on every axis averaged across orders and to be at or above the Stacks comparator on the six-axis mean averaged across orders; report each order as well. An order-reversing preference is inconclusive, not a win. In that case retain both lanes and ask the owner to adjudicate; do not retire the comparator. No score overrides a failed source read. This predeclared threshold is a proposed gate for the new method, not a result.

For revised released engines there **is** an original: freeze the current citation YAMLs/output conditions and compare revised versus original on the same source snapshot, Sonnet both orders, candidate mean at or above original, plus all source/anchor/cell gates. A marker rendition changes the evidence condition; isolate that comparison rather than attribute all benefit to the prompt.
