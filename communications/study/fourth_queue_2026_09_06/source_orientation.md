# Source orientation before output adjudication

These are source-side notes, not release findings or rubric scores. The validation uses the papers as supplied; reported historical and theoretical claims are not independently verified against unsupplied references. Codex read the argumentative bodies and relevant notes before judging generated outputs. Output-specific memos follow separately.

## Chen — G5, C5, S5

Chen reconstructs Jaeggi before criticizing her. The first movement distinguishes expansion and deepening within moralism, then historicism; note 2 explicitly says the historicism critique is a reconstruction from Jaeggi’s more indirect response. Note 11 separates Laudan as a resource for the priority of progress from Kuhn for crisis/revolution, and flags their disagreement. A citation map must preserve these mediated attributions, not certify Jaeggi’s fidelity to Kuhn from an unsupplied book.

The middle movement supplies forms of life, second-order crises, the mismatch between practices and interpretations, reform/revolution and experience enrichment. Regression is not simply failing to solve quickly: the quoted criterion requires systematic, self-caused blockage of access to crisis trends. The know-how reply is a possible defense that Chen develops and then challenges. It cannot be attributed to an unqualified concession by Chen or treated as entirely absent. Kuhn’s scientific community shares a puzzle-solving goal; Chen says social forms of life need not share such a goal. These claims remain Chen’s argumentative reconstruction.

Roth’s real-solution condition says a solution is progressive **only if** it creates no more serious or intractable problem; the direction is necessary, not sufficient. Chen’s marriage example then contests that criterion. The slavery/abolition and childhood examples distinguish abandoning a skill or experience from moral regression; no automatic equation of accumulation and progress is safe. In the alternative, Chen keeps reasons and empirical evidence while denying a neutral algorithm. The psychologist proposal is conditional on retaining the science/social parallelism, and the conclusion offers a plausible alternative, not a demonstrated consensus. Allen’s closing passage adds a forward-looking imperative without a self-congratulatory historical narrative. A learning route is a proposal grounded in these dependencies, with no observed learner outcomes.

## Castoriadis, Rationality — A7, I4, I9

The critique is explicitly immanent: capitalism is tested against its own criteria, not discredited merely because all societies institute their own values. Autonomy also remains within that institution/justification circle. Capitalism’s identification of welfare with an economic maximum and its monetary valuation of activity are attributed doctrines, not the author’s own unrestricted values.

The separability discussion says legal/private-property accounting boundaries are conventional while actual production depends on a fuzzy collectivity and accumulated history. It distinguishes identifying an entity from separating all its productive contributions. The later costs passage expressly excludes environmental destruction, flattened lives, ugly cities and other consequences from the calculation and says this kind of calculation cannot absorb them. A reframing toward society/history is supported; a complete social-cost computation is not.

For cooperation, the paper documents management-clan struggles, worker resistance and opacity, and aggregate investment’s dependence on uncertain expectations. It does not give a measured payoff matrix or establish every group shares one goal. Wage restraint and opposition to state demand regulation are source-reported conflicts; working-class struggles and political reactions force changes that the author says preserved demand and resilience. The author does not call all resistance free riding. The workplace note points to an earlier essay, which is not supplied in this validation; a detailed informal-shopfloor mechanism cannot be imported from Technique or another essay.

The long boom and later inflation/reaction retain chronology and modality. The final scenario says global companies might keep doing well while wider societies suffer, conditional on avoiding major political hitches; it is possible, not certain or necessarily most probable. The conclusion leaves outcomes to populations’ reactions and actions.

## Deutschmann, Promise — C4, T5

Money’s generalized capacity is not just purchasing goods: the Simmel reconstruction distinguishes material/imaginary goods, property and personal freedom, time and space. Its freedom-of-choice effect applies beyond subsistence needs; for the poor the relation becomes debt and pressure to sell labor. Money as a communicative medium and money as privately appropriable capacity are distinguished, as are metaphysical analogy and an actual causal mechanism.

Money transformed into capital is described through M–C–M′ and the growth imperative. The article then criticizes both a sufficient temporal-labor explanation of profit and Schumpeter’s heroic individual entrepreneur. Creative labor and entrepreneurial action are complementary; bureaucratic control cannot automatically mobilize creativity, workers must remain free, and workers, inventors, financiers, salesmen and politicians must cooperate. Institutionalized markets are distinct from money’s meta-market of possibilities.

The explicit myth cycle is generation, diffusion, institutionalization and decline. Successful communication stimulates imitation/diffusion; diffusion stimulates competition and new applications. Stabilization enables calculability and investment, but lock-in blocks alternatives and profitability declines; crisis/search and the old myth’s blind spots become the starting point of another myth. Each edge needs its own predicate before calling a path closed. The essay reports acceleration without a measured rate or fixed duration. The final market-radicalism passage says entrepreneurs may withdraw to financial/knowledge domains and that decoupling may be a symptom. The depressive spiral is conditional on creative destruction halting; the suggested disillusion is a prospective argument, not a completed event.

## Abels, Subsea — E3, I11, I13

The paper explicitly takes an intermediate position between statecraft and capital-centered accounts. It acknowledges successful state co-option in some instances while rejecting it as generally assured. Industrial, regulatory and coercive strategies are ideal types that actual cases combine. The two cases are deliberately instructive/prototypical/novel, not a representative causal-identification sample. The France case adds interviews because journalistic coverage is thinner; reported triangulation is not independent replication.

SeaMeWe-6: the consortium initially selected HMN Tech; its approximately $500m subsidized bid remained lower than SubCom’s reduced $600m offer. US financing/grants and sanctions threats both matter. The late-2021 vote changes the supplier; China Telecom and China Mobile withdraw about 20% of total investment, while China Unicom remains and replacement/existing investors fill the gap. An inferred diagnostic claim must allow mixed mechanisms and cannot call the price gap alone decisive causal identification.

PEACE was built by HMN Tech and became operational in 2022; it is owned by the named Hengtong subsidiary. EMA is the project explicitly described as following exclusion from SeaMeWe-6, with a reported $500m contract and future route. Do not transfer the reactive chronology, completion or contract to PEACE. Orange’s partner listing and EMA memorandum are different relations and statuses.

ASN: an attempted Orange/FSI intervention in 2012 reached no agreement. Nokia acquired Alcatel-Lucent in 2016. Nokia signaled a possible ASN sale in summer 2023; negotiations began June 2024 and a deal was struck in November. The French state via APE would acquire 80% for €350m; remaining 20% purchase is an intention after transition. Later text calls ASN now nationalized. The report thus supports agreed majority acquisition/nationalized status, with no exact closing day or evidence that the future 20% has transferred. Calais/seven ships and Orange Marine’s role are distinct attributes; projected revenue growth is a forecast. Network inscriptions include actual reviews, decrees and route maps; a device’s presence alone does not establish enrollment or an obligatory passage point. Source caveats include fiscal limits, cable-financing dependence and pressure from US sanctions.

## Harris — R2

Harris’s organizing demand is to abandon repair of Honneth’s recognition theory and seek alternative foundations. He constructs a contrast with first-generation critical theory, argues that monism is definitional, then accumulates eight objections. Recurring claims that a theory fails while claiming success, vivid examples, collective identification with critical theorists and the label recognition theory light are observable rhetorical operations. The scholarly adoption/reception claims are Harris’s reports, not this study’s independent evidence of audience response.

The strongest qualifying passages matter: recognition relations exist and can be important within capitalism; a minimal recognition approach could be a small component of a different theory; his initial reconstruction calls The Struggle for Recognition remarkable and explains its plural conception of self-realization. The conclusion accepts that he uses Honneth productively on critical theory’s legacy and can be unduly assertive. Note 2 acknowledges development from philosophical anthropology to a more right-Hegelian view of institutions, and note 5 acknowledges The Working Sovereign’s more explicit labor engagement while maintaining criticism. A persuasive reading must retain these qualifications alongside the unsalvageable-theory framing. Harris’s Fanon passages are attributed interpretations and rhetorical invitations; they do not certify Fanon’s full position or actual audience effects.
