The three-row, five-criterion matrix correctly compares conceptual approaches. It does not construct three national experiments or convert bibliographic dates into a linear intellectual development. It separates each defining object, ends/means relationship, institutions and power, collective agency, and diagnosis or alternative. Its strongest similarity—socially instituted ends and criteria—does not erase differences among technology, democratic legitimacy and capitalist valuation. Cultural term-to-term comparison, the meaning of techne, democratic scale and economic comparability are explicit limits.

The exception tests are a substantive strength. Narrow technical efficiency does not establish the neutrality of an entire technical ensemble. Liberal oligarchy is not equated to a totalitarian regime because rights and liberties are real. Criticism of capitalist rationality does not deny productive capacity; the account preserves working-class and state constraints as part of historical resilience. The democratic economy includes genuine consumer choice/markets and producer self-management. This avoids the most predictable distortions of these sources. The output offers no quantitative ranking or claimed demonstrated success of its proposed alternatives.

Evidence granularity still needs attention. The technique alternative cell cites F5, whose anchor only denies neutrality; it does not anchor the constructive work-process proposal. F10 states self-management and collective economic decisions while quoting only consumer sovereignty. F11 combines quantitative rationality with instituted products and excluded costs while quoting only maximization/minimization. F14's matrix cell includes theoretical incoherence, precarious work and rising profits while the retained finding and quote only report high unemployment. The sources contain relevant passages, but the table-to-finding route does not carry the whole cell. F18's 'not comparable' fragment needs the named quantities and the source's qualification that limited qualitative aggregate comparisons remain possible. The prose sometimes explains technically inconclusive scope records through the conceptual genre's lack of standardized indicators, although that genre limitation is not itself a failure to compare approaches.

They should have stayed per-document findings. F17 cites corpus lineage CM.F2 while retaining only a Democracy anchor. All final F-number citations exist, but some point to these incomplete corpus rows.
