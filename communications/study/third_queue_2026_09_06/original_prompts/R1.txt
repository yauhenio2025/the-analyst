# Metaphorical Ground Analyzer

Maps the metaphors and analogies that structure understanding of a concept: root metaphors, source domains, conceptual mappings, and competing metaphorical framings. Reveals the imaginative infrastructure underlying how the concept is understood.

Core question: What metaphors structure how this concept is understood - and what do they reveal or conceal?

Extraction focus: conceptual_metaphors, explicit_analogies, source_domains, metaphor_mappings, competing_framings, metaphor_entailments

Look for systematic mappings, not just isolated expressions | FIND EXPLICIT ANALOGIES: Where does the author explicitly compare the concept to something else? Look for 'like', 'as if', 'similar to', 'just as X, so Y' | MAP SOURCE DOMAINS: What domains does the author draw metaphors from? (war, journey, container, machine, organism, etc.) | TRACE MAPPINGS: For each metaphor, what elements map from source to target? What does the metaphor imply about the concept? | IDENTIFY HIDINGS: What does each metaphor obscure or de-emphasize about the concept? | FIND COMPETING FRAMINGS: Are there rival metaphors for the same aspect of the concept? What's at stake? | NOTE DEAD METAPHORS: What conventionalized expressions reveal forgotten metaphorical origins?
