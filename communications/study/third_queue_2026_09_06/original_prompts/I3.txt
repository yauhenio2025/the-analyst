# Incentive Structure Mapper

Deep analysis of what behavior systems actually reward. Reveals the hidden incentives that drive actors, the principal-agent problems that create dysfunction, the collective action traps that make rational individuals produce irrational outcomes, and the perverse incentives that make well-intentioned policies backfire. Uses sophisticated methods from public choice, game theory, and institutional economics but outputs in plain language anyone can understand.

Core question: What does this system actually reward, and why do people act the way they do?

Extraction focus: incentive_structures, principal_agent_problems, collective_action_problems, game_structures, perverse_incentives, gaming_opportunities, rent_seeking, behavioral_predictions

Steps: IDENTIFY ACTORS: Who are the key players? What are their interests, resources, constraints? What do they know? What's their time horizon? | MAP INCENTIVE STRUCTURES: For each actor, what behaviors are rewarded? How? What's measured? What's punished? | PREDICT BEHAVIORS: Given incentives, what will actors rationally do? How does this differ from intended behavior? | FIND PRINCIPAL-AGENT PROBLEMS: Where do information asymmetries create moral hazard or adverse selection? Who's the principal? Who's the agent? What can't the principal observe? | FIND COLLECTIVE ACTION PROBLEMS: Where do individual rationality and collective rationality diverge? Public goods? Commons? Coordination failures? Free rider problems? | ANALYZE GAME STRUCTURES: What games are actors playing? What's the payoff structure? Where's the Nash equilibrium? Is cooperation possible? | IDENTIFY PERVERSE INCENTIVES: Where do incentives backfire? Where does rewarding X produce less of X or more of Y? | FIND GAMING OPPORTUNITIES: How can the system be gamed? What metrics can be manipulated? Who's gaming? | IDENTIFY RENT-SEEKING: Where are actors extracting value without creating it? What political economy sustains this? | ASSESS ALIGNMENT: How well do incentives serve stated goals? Where's the misalignment? | TRACE OUTCOMES: What behaviors produce what outcomes? Who benefits? Who loses? | PROPOSE REDESIGNS: How could incentives be restructured? What gaming would the new structure invite?
