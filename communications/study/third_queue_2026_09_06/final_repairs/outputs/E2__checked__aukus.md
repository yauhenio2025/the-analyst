### Counter-evidence

The article itself supplies qualifications to some broad formulations. “Relative bipartisanship” does not mean frictionless implementation: Republican senators reportedly tried to block submarine-sale legislation, and the article separately reports uncertainty arising from US and UK submarine capacity [F11]. This is a difference between general political support and conditions on a particular transfer, not a demonstrated contradiction.

No supplied original contradicts the article’s financial figures. Because the originals are absent, the correct status is **unverified**, not false. In particular, the uncited USD 1.28 billion loan claim cannot be treated as corroborated by Ker (2024) merely because Ker closes the preceding Lynas sentence [F4] [F5].
