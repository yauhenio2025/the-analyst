## Method boundary and ideal outputs

Engagement becomes **citation_engagement_map**, a separate corpus method. G5 v2 **citation_invocation_map** asks about the citation uses of one supplied text. An A→P engagement reading fixes the two identities, inventories P's role in each A text, then predicates continuity or change across dated texts. These have different input scopes, row identities and comparison warrants. No released G5 question is edited or superseded.

The ideal engagement output is a bibliographic reading, a passage × cited object × move × stance × locus inventory, a trajectory by text/year and a short route into the sources. Its five document dimensions are place in the argument, cited object, argumentative move, stance and passage reading. Its corpus dimension aligns P's place across A's supplied work with paired anchors.

**citation_fidelity_audit** implements G6 from ACCURACY_SYSTEM. The ideal output places A's actual attribution beside P's own words at the retrieved place, assigns attribution kind and a reasoned verdict, and states edition, locus and checked-sample coverage. Five document dimensions establish attribution kind, A's claim, P's position, surrounding qualifications and edition/locus limits. The verdict is a corpus dimension: although its unit is one citation pair, its predicate relates two source documents. Declaring it corpus scope makes the existing two-key wall require A/anchor and W/anchor-b; combining both sides into an artificial single source would lose this guarantee. A second corpus dimension describes only the checked sample. Unverifiable and unheld cases remain explicit; irrelevant search results cannot establish misattribution. Local extraction defers the verdict until both source-side readings exist.

**citation_reception_map** implements G8 from RECEPTION_SYSTEM. The ideal output is reader × theme × position plus A among the supplied readers. Its five document dimensions are reader argument, stance, theme positions, documented citation of A and date/coverage/count authority. The corpus dimension distinguishes consonance, explicit opposition, interpretive contrast and no demonstrated engagement. A's texts are the reference point. Primary windows and generated indexes are not secondary readers. Similarity does not prove influence; later publication cannot be something an earlier A failed to engage. A most-cited uncited list must come from supplied citation counts. Local name-match counts cannot substitute for scholarly citation counts.

## Predicate rule and execution

Every dimension carries a concrete answer shape and an imperative method card. The supporting spans must contain the actor, predicate, object and necessary qualification. A matched name is not a supported reading. Separate spans are separate anchors, with distinct source keys on relational rows. Index quotations do not become independent primary evidence. Voice, coauthorship, reported attribution, negation, edition and chronology remain attached to their claims. The LLM reads, interprets, critiques and reconciles; walls check only anchors and IDs.

Each capability has an operationalization with `process:`. Surface uses oneshot, standard uses oneshot_checked, deep/dvs uses extraction → verification → synthesis. Routing is Luna / DeepSeek V4 Pro / Sol. All three begin behind the catalog exclusion, including ordinary chosen-path resolution. The shared `UNDER_THE_SHAPE` checks now include the family, and focused tests cover pair source identity and the corpus builder's pagination/reference behavior.
