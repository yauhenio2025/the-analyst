## Explicit controls to freeze with the harness

The following are authored test artifacts, not quotations or genuine papers. Give them the shared fictional provenance “Archive Working Group — validation fixture,” with exact source keys as shown. Save their bodies verbatim in the new campaign; their synthetic status belongs in the manifest and report. The controls exercise the eligibility boundary and reporting contract, not scholarly generalization.

**`archive_inventory` — standalone absence control and mixed-corpus document B:**

> Inventory note. The catalogue lists three items: A17, a blue folder; A18, a grey folder; A19, a green folder. The entries give identifiers and physical descriptions. Shelf 4 contains these three items. The catalogue date is 5 September 2026.

Expected review question: does the engine invent an organizing opposition or consequential supposition from mere enumeration? A supported negative must state the inspected note and eligibility criterion. Positive volume earns no credit. The original condition may express an honest negative in prose without the candidate's technical fields; judge its substance fairly.

**`archive_policy` — mixed-corpus document A:**

> Policy note. Original records should remain unchanged so that an audit can reconstruct the decisions recorded in them. Personal addresses should be removed from public copies. These requirements concern different copies: the original remains under restricted access, and a redacted copy is released. The distinction permits both requirements to be met. Had every original been replaced by its redacted copy, an auditor could not recover the removed addresses from the archive, although the remaining dates and decisions would still be available. This loss would impair that part of the audit, not eliminate every form of accountability.

Expected review question: can the engine recover qualified compatibility, a reasoned counterfactual, a specified loss and surviving information without upgrading the claims? At least one positive finding is plausible; the exact number or analytical wording is not prescribed. Conceptual support must not be recast as an unsupported empirical frequency claim.

**`archive_fragment` — mixed-corpus document C:**

> Incomplete argument fragment. The preceding page, which is not supplied, states the two requirements and the exception on which the conclusion depends. The next sentence reads: “For that reason the exception resolves their conflict.” No further explanation is present in this supplied fragment.

Expected review question: does the output preserve missing context? The transition's basis cannot be reconstructed from this artifact. An inconclusive assessment should remain visible for the unresolved scope. A carefully scoped negative about what the supplied fragment itself establishes is assessable; an unqualified claim that the full argument has no relevant instance is not. The fixture is not an empty or failed source load: its limitation is part of the available text and provenance.

The mixed corpus must keep A's positive evidence, B's scoped negative and C's uncertainty separately. A cross-document relation is not required merely to fill the corpus dimension. If one is claimed, both source-keyed anchors and the comparative warrant must survive. Missing source, malformed JSON and lost-anchor behavior are already tested with fake models; the paid controls must not deliberately create transport failures or spend money to retest parser branches.

## Primary reading evidence and secondary scoring

Follow the frozen [pre-result reading plan](STUDY_argument_family_READING_PLAN_2026-09-05.md). Read all sixteen initial products against their sources, save an initial memo snapshot and output hashes, then review the eight corpus products. Freeze complete source-aware memos covering all 24 actual finals before opening either rater's scores. Assess attribution, scope/modality, warrant, analytical value, independently surviving routes, prose/ledger consistency, and negative/inconclusive outcomes. A verified quotation proves occurrence, not interpretation. Keep parser failures, carried unchecked findings and missing context distinct from substantive mistakes.

Every reading is independently scored by Sonnet 4.6 and Sol against the same full source(s), actual final product, and neutral description of its engine task. A call contains one reading only: no competing product, earlier scores, reading memo, condition label or requested preference. Visible receipts and formats can still reveal treatment or model family; this is not full blinding. The two engines' neutral task lines, exact shared rubric and model routes are frozen in the manifest.

Use exactly the original frontier's six criteria: **specificity, anchoring, non_obviousness, coherence, usefulness, hallucination_risk**. All use 1–10, higher better; hallucination_risk=10 means no unsupported claims. Require one concise source-specific reason for each and a one-line assessment. Do not introduce score bands, default missing values or partial means. Grounded scoped negatives and uncertainty can score well; prose and structured records receive the same standard. Novelty is relative to the source, and neither length nor check status earns credit.

Report each rater's six scores, unweighted mean and original-to-candidate deltas separately, along with Sonnet–Sol differences on each identical output. Keep single papers, natural corpora, absence controls and mixed controls distinct. No pooled rater mean or automated win count replaces source reading. Historical frontier scores show model-family associations, but identity, quality and presentation were not independently manipulated; they do not establish a causal family bias or an unbiased rater. Independent scoring removes the within-call A/B alternative, not every possible bias.

**Optional corroboration:** reserve at most one pair, evaluated in both orders, only if the two raters give opposing original-to-candidate mean gaps of at least 1.5 points each. Use unrounded means for admission. This is a restrictive administrative threshold, not statistical significance. Root must first review the disagreement against the sources and bind all 72 prerequisite outputs plus a new memo in a separate `corroborate` record. Eligibility alone makes no call. Small or aligned gaps do not trigger comparison. Both orders must agree to support a preference; splits, ties and incomplete judgments remain explicit. Such a result never overrides the primary memos. The user was offered the option to omit this step; no reply was received before freezing this restrictive default.

The corpus audit must inspect actual extraction, verification and synthesis handoffs for every document and the corpus dimension. Report input availability separately from final coverage; inspect paired anchors, lineage, retained/dropped/missing claims and all positive/negative/inconclusive scopes. A final anchored only in one document cannot establish complete corpus coverage. Positive ratings alone do not authorize production registration: unresolved material errors, honest controls, usable desk source bindings and remaining limits require explicit review.
