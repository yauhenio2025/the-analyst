# Argument-family validation: concrete no-call plan

**Prepared, not launched.** This plan concerns the two [unregistered candidates](candidates/argument_family_2026_09_05/README.md), `dialectical_structure` and `counterfactual_analyzer`. It neither changes production registration nor adds work or spend to the separate four-engine Hegel trial. Its proposed admission ceiling is **USD 16**, requiring separate launch authorization after a frozen harness preview. The conservative preparation estimate below is **USD 13.63**; it is not a billing guarantee.

The accepted scoped-outcome runtime makes these candidates executable for an isolated comparison. It does not certify their questions, semantic judgments or reported inspection coverage. Root's source-review feedback has been added as general critic guidance: identify the kind of warrant, allow grounded analytical reconstruction, distinguish inadequate treatment from absence, and preserve the actual quantified/modal claim when testing a counterexample. Paper-specific checks below stay outside the models' prompts.

No order is presumed between the two 2001 papers. Missing provenance for the 2022 translation and OCR limitations remain explicit.

These sources have informed candidate design and earlier reviews; they are **not held-out generalization evidence for this new family**. Fresh reading memos must assess actual complete candidate and original outputs before judge responses are opened. The checks are discriminating probes, not an exhaustive answer key: a useful unforeseen interpretation remains assessable against the source.

## Explicit controls to freeze with the harness

The following are authored test artifacts, not quotations or genuine papers. Give them the shared fictional provenance “Archive Working Group — validation fixture,” with exact source keys as shown. Save their bodies verbatim in the new campaign; their synthetic status belongs in the manifest and report. The controls exercise the eligibility boundary and reporting contract, not scholarly generalization.

**`archive_inventory` — standalone absence control and mixed-corpus document B:**

> Inventory note. The catalogue lists three items: A17, a blue folder; A18, a grey folder; A19, a green folder. The entries give identifiers and physical descriptions. Shelf 4 contains these three items. The catalogue date is 5 September 2026.

Expected review question: does the engine invent an organizing opposition or consequential supposition from mere enumeration? A supported negative must state the inspected note and eligibility criterion. Positive volume earns no credit. The original condition may express an honest negative in prose without the candidate's technical fields; judge its substance fairly.

**`archive_policy` — mixed-corpus document A:**

> Policy note. Original records should remain unchanged so that an audit can reconstruct the decisions recorded in them. Personal addresses should be removed from public copies. These requirements concern different copies: the original remains under restricted access, and a redacted copy is released. The distinction permits both requirements to be met. Had every original been replaced by its redacted copy, an auditor could not recover the removed addresses from the archive, although the remaining dates and decisions would still be available. This loss would impair that part of the audit, not eliminate every form of accountability.

Expected review question: can the engine recover qualified compatibility, a reasoned counterfactual, a specified loss and surviving information without upgrading the claims? At least one positive finding is plausible; the exact number or analytical wording is not prescribed. Conceptual support must not be recast as an unsupported empirical frequency claim.

**`archive_fragment` — mixed-corpus document C:**

> Incomplete argument fragment. The preceding page, which is not supplied, states the two requirements and the exception on which the conclusion depends. The next sentence reads: “For that reason the exception resolves their conflict.” No further explanation is present in this supplied fragment.

Expected review question: does the output preserve missing context? The transition's basis cannot be reconstructed from this artifact. An inconclusive assessment should remain visible for the unresolved scope. A carefully scoped negative about what the supplied fragment itself establishes is assessable; an unqualified claim that the full argument has no relevant instance is not. The fixture is not an empty or failed source load: its limitation is part of the available text and provenance.

The mixed corpus must keep A's positive evidence, B's scoped negative and C's uncertainty separately. A cross-document relation is not required merely to fill the corpus dimension. If one is claimed, both source-keyed anchors and the comparative warrant must survive. Missing source, malformed JSON and lost-anchor behavior are already tested with fake models; the paid controls must not deliberately create transport failures or spend money to retest parser branches.

## Review, judging and exposure decision

Freeze a source-aware reader memo for each engine before opening judge results. Review the full source as necessary; inspected sections and limits remain reader reports rather than assertions inferred from file length. Assess attribution, scope/modality, warrant type, analytical value, independent surviving routes, prose/ledger consistency, and the honesty of negative/inconclusive outcomes. A verified quotation establishes occurrence, not interpretive correctness. Distinguish model interpretation from a parser failure, unchecked carried row, or incomplete source.

For each complete pair, Sonnet 4.6 receives the same sources and actual final products twice, once original=A/candidate=B and once reversed. Use a shared, source-aware rubric fixed before generation. It should judge specificity, grounded reconstruction, coherence and usefulness; it should explicitly allow honest scoped negative or inconclusive results and avoid rewarding row count, length, or technical receipt format. This rubric adjustment is shared by both conditions and must be hashed. The original ideas study's judge rubric remains preserved as provenance, not silently overwritten.

Normalize A/B verdicts to the fixed condition identities. Only two valid opposite-order judgments agreeing on a winner count as a win; two ties count as a tie. Split votes, a win/tie pair, malformed judgments and missing judgments remain separately reported. Never remap unknown output IDs to rescue a verdict or a critic ruling. Visible receipts and differing formats can reveal treatment; order reversal does not produce full blinding.

The same-author corpus audit must inspect actual extraction, verification and synthesis handoffs for all documents and the corpus dimension, not just count final quote matches. Report final document coverage separately from input availability. Check both paired anchors, trimming diagnostics, lineage, retained/dropped/missing anchors and the final scope inventory. A final mentioning only one document cannot be called complete corpus validation merely because every displayed anchor matches.

The reader memo, both-order judgments, raw/mechanical diagnostics and cost table form separate evidence. Positive judgments alone do not authorize exposure. Before registration, require an explicit source review of unresolved material errors, absence controls without forced instances, mixed scopes preserved, usable source bindings on the desk, and a report of remaining limits. This plan establishes no arbitrary universal pass score or inference of broad reliability from twelve pairs.

## Conservative estimate and launch controls

Calibration uses only the completed baseline [results](../../data/study/ideas_2026_09_05/374325c24e6b10a1/results.json) and its saved per-call receipts, without new price lookup or provider calls. Across 24 read calls, 12 checks and 24 judgments, the largest observed per-call costs were Sol **USD 0.100878**, DeepSeek **0.053918** and Sonnet **0.099351**. The largest of four complete 21-call natural corpora cost **USD 0.7132**. Corresponding role totals were **1.677656**, **0.521615**, **1.959600**; corpus jobs ranged **0.5902–0.7132**. These are receipt costs, not current provider quotes.

For planning, use 1.5 times each observed maximum. Natural-corpus original reads receive an additional 4× multiplier and natural-corpus judgments 3×, to allow substantially longer source context; these are deliberately conservative approximations, not exact prompt estimates. The tiny synthetic mixed corpus still gets the full 1.5× natural deep-job allowance. This also leaves room for the new scope JSON and report overhead in a way a direct prior average would not.

The budget is an admission guard: in-flight output length, provider retries and unknown usage can exceed an estimate. Do not claim a guaranteed billing cap.

The new harness must default to no-call preview and bind runtime/composer/walls/normalization, both candidate YAMLs, untouched capability baselines, model routing, source/control bytes, judge rubric and all shared contracts. Every actual dynamic prompt, raw response, token/cost record, requested/used model, partial/error metadata and lineage must be retained. Freeze the accepted runtime including ruling-coverage and scope-outcome behavior. A default source binding or permissively dropped metadata is not a valid frozen treatment.

Use one separate campaign identity and cumulative spend ledger covering its accepted continuations. Admit every invocation against known spend plus its conservative envelope; unknown-cost calls block further paid work. Do not retry or resume a failed/running job into new model calls automatically. Any permitted unstarted continuation must retain frozen conditions and provenance; offline recovery must be separately recorded and cannot silently change treatment or guess IDs. Stop after the initial single-paper and absence generations for root's source/format review before the more expensive corpus phase. Judge only actual completed products after the pre-judge memos are saved. No command in this plan starts a model call.
