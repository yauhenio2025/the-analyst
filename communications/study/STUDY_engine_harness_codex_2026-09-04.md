# Does the engine harness earn its keep? — Codex reader memo (2026-09-04)

**Verdict: not in its present form.** On this material the full harness loses every primary blind comparison, while taking four calls and producing much more prose. I would keep the analytical methods, but replace the 200-plus-engine/fixed-pass apparatus with roughly a dozen well-defined method families: a strong-model, structured, source-anchored one-shot by default; a targeted critic and final synthesis only when the planning model judges that the material warrants them. The exception worth preserving is the conditions engine's final integration: it contains a synthesis the plain one-shot did not quite reach. That is evidence for an optional second call, not for the present four-pass transcript.

## 1. Does the harness beat the one-shot?

No, as evaluated. The twelve primary pairwise judgments (six match-ups repeated with A/B order reversed) all choose the plain or questions one-shot over the harness, always by a clear margin (`data/study/judgments.json:174-302`). My reading agrees about the full outputs. The one-shot argument map gets immediately to the Toulmin skeleton, grounds, scheme weaknesses and six suppressed premises, ending with the usable distinction “successful re-description, unsuccessful causal thesis” (`data/study/outputs/argument_architecture__oneshot__fable.md:3-15,25-37,53-68`). The one-shot conditions analysis likewise makes its decisive finding—the undeclared definition of neoliberalism—on line 5 and develops one linear argument (`data/study/outputs/conditions_of_possibility_analyzer__oneshot_questions__fable.md:3-21,35-43,61-71`).

The harness does find some additional value. Conditions pass 3 notices the exact causal inversion—regulation is “motivated by geopolitics,” although geopolitics is supposed to be surface—and proposes a sequential capture thesis (`data/study/outputs/conditions_of_possibility_analyzer__harness__fable.md:93-99`). Its integration then sees the definitional gap and causal inversion as “one hole” and finds a possible repair in the paper's own market-construction vocabulary (`data/study/outputs/conditions_of_possibility_analyzer__harness__fable.md:201-211`). Argument integration identifies the Cold War analogy as evidence for the rival security-first account and the VC/Pentagon advocacy clause as the best undeveloped causal evidence (`data/study/outputs/argument_architecture__harness__sonnet.md:561-569,583-605`). Those gains plausibly come from confrontation applied to prior findings and then integration—not from “deep” as such, and not from lineage, which is not supplied. The probing questions improve coverage, but the questions-only outputs still beat the harness and do not establish that questions outperform the plain prompt.

The cost is large. On Sonnet, conditions took 479.2 rather than 77 seconds and emitted 95,121 rather than 13,713 characters; argument took 498.9 rather than 106 seconds and emitted 100,123 rather than 17,815 characters (`data/study/manifest.json:2-22,68-88`). All recorded dollar costs are zero, so this study cannot honestly quantify money; four repeated full-document calls would normally cost more, but that is an inference. Coherence suffers because discovery is explicitly told not to compress or organize for a consumer (`src/operations/definitions/stances.yaml:21-40`), and later passes narrate and repeat the process. Fable exposes a worse failure: early pass bodies are empty and later passes reconstruct them (`data/study/outputs/argument_architecture__harness__fable.md:1-17`; `data/study/outputs/conditions_of_possibility_analyzer__harness__fable.md:1-19`).

One qualification: the blind judge saw concatenated pass transcripts, whereas the dossier stores only the last pass as a phase's `final_output` (`src/dossier/analysis.py:122-143`). The integration-only conditions outputs are coherent and valuable (`data/study/outputs/conditions_of_possibility_analyzer__harness_integration_only__fable.md:41-87`). Thus the experiment decisively rejects shipping/reading all passes, but is less decisive about a hidden scratch pass followed by one final product.

## 2. Is multi-pass the right structure?

Not this structure. In argument architecture, pass 1 already states the master enthymeme, circularity, counterfactual gap and nine ranked vulnerabilities (`data/study/outputs/argument_architecture__harness__sonnet.md:149-171,223-233`). Pass 2 formalizes burden and suppressed commitments; pass 3 supplies the best adversarial tests; pass 4 largely rephrases them as “illumination versus explanation” (`data/study/outputs/argument_architecture__harness__sonnet.md:551-581`). In conditions analysis, pass 2's debt/path map and pass 3's causal inversion are real additions, while pass 4 is the useful consumer artifact. A good single prompt could perform “map, test the strongest rival, then conclude” internally; the study shows no need to materialize each cognitive verb as a separate call.

`consumes_from` sends whole prior prose, untruncated, to later inner passes (`src/executor/context_broker.py:108-152`); both deep sequences make integration consume every prior pass (`src/operationalizations/definitions/conditions_of_possibility_analyzer.yaml:117-136`; `src/engines/capability_definitions/argument_architecture.yaml:602-623`). It should carry a compact finding ledger, counterevidence and unresolved questions, not research diaries. There is also an implementation defect: runtime recomposition creates a `PassDefinition` with `description=""`, so the operationalized pass description is dropped (`src/executor/chain_runner.py:357-373`), despite the composer otherwise placing that description in the prompt (`src/stages/capability_composer.py:327-360`).

Depth is mechanically real but analytically unvalidated: it selects a pass sequence and dimension guidance (`src/stages/capability_composer.py:247-270,413-439`) and can affect model routing (`src/executor/engine_runner.py:141-149`). But “deep” mainly bundles more passes with words such as “every,” “full,” and “exhaustive.” No surface/standard/deep comparison was run, so quality depth is speculation. Worse, the conditions YAML advertises three deep passes while the overriding operationalization executes four (`src/engines/capability_definitions/conditions_of_possibility_analyzer.yaml:602-665`; `src/operationalizations/definitions/conditions_of_possibility_analyzer.yaml:117-136`). It is presently a cost/configuration dial, not a calibrated depth dial.

## 3. Definitions

**Conditions of possibility.** Enabling versus constraining conditions are distinct and useful. Path dependencies add causal order. But unacknowledged debts overlaps cross-domain transfer; alternative paths overlaps the central counterfactual; synthetic judgment is an output stage, not an analytical dimension (`src/engines/capability_definitions/conditions_of_possibility_analyzer.yaml:124-235`). The questions are expert-sounding but invite unsupported intellectual biography—motives for non-citation, reputational stakes and “proportion” estimates—when only one target paper is present. That mismatch matters because deep is declared suitable for prolific authors with 10+ works (`src/engines/capability_definitions/conditions_of_possibility_analyzer.yaml:602-607`), yet the harness confidently infers networks and concealed ancestry. Missing are evidence-status rules, historical discontinuity, power/knowledge and Skinner's constraint against attributing intentions beyond the available linguistic context.

**Argument architecture.** Structural composition and scheme identification are load-bearing. Suppressed elements substantially duplicate implicit warrants already required by structural composition; attack/defence partly duplicates quality assessment; legal standards such as “beyond reasonable doubt” are generic and ill-fitted to this journal article (`src/engines/capability_definitions/argument_architecture.yaml:149-267`). Missing are stable claim IDs, source spans, a charitable rival reconstruction, and scheme-specific critical questions applied only after a scheme is evidenced.

Lineage should enter both prompts as a compact **method card**, not a prestige paragraph or content-bearing worked example. The YAML actually contains operational methods—Toulmin decomposition and Walton matching followed by critical questions (`src/engines/capability_definitions/argument_architecture.yaml:268-323`)—but composition emits only the problematique plus names of traditions and key concepts (`src/stages/capability_composer.py:384-410`). A conditions card should say “identify historically evidenced rules of sayability; distinguish influence from analogy; label debts and motives as hypotheses.” An argument card should say “anchor each claim/ground; reconstruct the strongest faithful warrant; apply the detected scheme's critical questions; distinguish consistency from causal support.”

## 4. Process and workflows

Engine output matters upstream of almost everything: the spine reads analysis prose (`src/dossier/spine.py:199-218`), commissioned tables read it (`src/dossier/tables.py:192-205`), figures derive their material from it (`src/dossier/figures.py:127-129,383-396`), and composition reads it again (`src/dossier/compose.py:293-301`). Prose is therefore the wrong canonical handoff. It makes each desk rediscover claims and anchors, and `analysis_prose` truncates each phase at 80,000 characters (`src/dossier/common.py:183-191`).

The canonical artifact should be model-authored structured findings: `finding_id`, concise claim, method/move, exact quote plus document/location, direct/inferred/speculative status, counterevidence, limitation, confidence rationale and executive implication. A prose rendering can coexist. Typed shape constrains form without stealing judgment from the model, exactly as the LLM-first doctrine permits (`~/projects/veo2/LLM-FIRST-DOCTRINE.md:287-303`). First change the handoff; then make the judge compare final consumer artifacts under equal budgets, checking evidential entailment, redundancy and executive utility. Code should verify quote membership and lifecycle only; the model should judge meaning.

## 5. The set-up as a whole

The 275/28/22 split says the registry is cataloguing imagined lenses, not reliable executable products. These two “engines” already decompose into reusable methods—genealogical/contextual analysis, Toulmin mapping, scheme testing, adversarial criticism, synthesis—with parameters. Fable's forced-tool incompatibility and five empty deep-path attempts further mean “offered” is not portable capability (`communications/study/STUDY_BRIEF_engine_harness_2026-09-04.md:34-36`; `data/study/manifest.json:90-113`).

I would invest in a smaller, better-defined method set and a strong-model one-shot plus the desk's anchor/shape walls. Multi-pass should be an LLM-chosen exception for a diagnosed need, not a registry-authored ritual. That follows the governing principle that the model judges relevance, quality and what happens next while code owns shape, sequence, arithmetic and persistence (`~/projects/veo2/LLM-FIRST-DOCTRINE.md:14-21,53-95`).

## 6. Ranked proposal

1. **Make anchored findings the engine contract.** Where: both capability YAMLs, `capability_composer.py`, dossier `common.py` and consumers. Why: downstream desks currently reread/truncate prose; one-shots win on anchoring. Test: same corpus, blind final-dossier comparison; measure supported-claim rate, anchor entailment, re-discovery disagreements and cost.
2. **Default to one analytical call; let a planning model request one targeted critic/integration call.** Where: `src/dossier/plan.py`, `src/dossier/common.py`, the two pass definitions/operationalization. Why: four calls add a few insights but full outputs lose every primary comparison. Test: preregister equal output budgets across one-shot, one-shot+critic and current harness on 12 varied texts.
3. **Fix handoff plumbing and failure semantics.** Where: `chain_runner.py:326-373`, `context_broker.py:108-152`. Preserve pass descriptions; pass the finding ledger; treat empty content as a mechanical failed call to retry then pause, never as valid context. Test: fake-provider tests for empty pass, resume, exact `consumes_from`, and prompt snapshots.
4. **Judge the artifact the dossier consumes.** Where: study harness, `src/dossier/crosscheck.py`, and proposed `src/dossier/readthrough.py`. Why: concatenated transcripts confound method quality with scratch verbosity, while last-pass argument synthesis still needs grounding. Test: blind, order-reversed comparisons of final outputs, with the source visible, equal word limits, calibrated human spot checks and real token-cost receipts.
5. **Collapse the registry into method families plus backend capability metadata.** Where: `src/engines/registry.py`, `src/dossier/common.py`, capability definitions. Why: 275 labels obscure 28 executable and backend failures; overlapping dimensions are parameters, not products. Test: map all offered engines to 10–15 families, then require each family to beat the generic anchored baseline on multiple genres and on every advertised backend before exposure.
