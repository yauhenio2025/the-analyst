## The three methods
1. **Engagement map** (A on P; corpus method). Documents: A's texts that cite or name P. Document dimensions: P's place in this text; per passage the thing cited, the move (authority · evidence · foil · dialogue · genealogy · illustration · courtesy · self-positioning), the stance (adopts · builds on · qualifies · disputes · mentions), the reading, the anchor. Corpus dimension: P's place across A's work and its trajectory by year, paired anchors. Tables: passage × move × stance × locus; trajectory by text and year.
2. **Citation fidelity audit** (G6; corpus method over pairs). Documents: for each passage of A that cites a specific work W of P at a locus, the passage (from A) and the page window of W (from the held copy) — supplied as an **evidence index** (see materials). Document dimensions per pair: attribution kind (direct · reported · contrast · none); what A attributes (anchor in A); what P says there (anchor in W, verbatim); verdict accurate · fair · selective · stretched · misattributed · unverifiable, with the reason; edition/locus notes. Corpus dimension: A's citation practice across the checked sample with the sample's coverage stated (never generalised beyond it). The two-key wall applies (anchor in A, anchor-b in W).
3. **Reception map** (G8; corpus method). Documents: held secondary texts on P (plus A's texts as the reference point). Document dimensions per reader: argument about P, stance (defends · reconstructs · criticizes · applies · surveys), position on each theme the engagement turns on with an anchor, whether it cites A. Corpus dimension: the readings A is consonant with, argues against, or never engages; the most-cited citers A does not cite comes from counts, not a model. Tables: reader × theme × position; A among P's readers.
