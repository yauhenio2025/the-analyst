# Study memo — does the engine harness earn its keep? (reader: fable, 2026-09-04)

**Verdict.** The harness earns its keep on one of the two engines and not the other, and the 12/12 loss in `data/study/judgments.json` is an artefact of what was judged, not of the reading. The judge was handed the four passes concatenated (40–100K chars); production hands the desks only the last pass (`src/dossier/analysis.py:139`, `final_output = content` "the last pass is the phase output"). When the fairness check judged that last pass alone (`data/study/judgments_integration_only.json`, 8 pairings), the split is clean: `conditions_of_possibility_analyzer` wins 4/4 (both models, both orders, "clear"); `argument_architecture` loses 4/4. My own reading of the four Fable outputs against `source_aukus.txt` agrees with that split and can explain it from the code: the multi-pass structure compounds for a genealogical/judgment method and does nothing for a mapping method, and two prompt-composition accidents make argument_architecture's final pass write for a machine. So: do not keep investing in a 275-engine registry, and do not retreat to a bare one-shot either. Invest in a small set of engines whose *kind* decides whether they run as one call with probing questions (mapping, extraction) or as passes (synthesis, judgment); fix three cheap prompt bugs that cost the harness most of its handoff quality; and give every pass a model-declared, wall-checked findings ledger so the spine can cite it. Detail and evidence below; where I speculate I say so.

## 0. Two facts about the evidence

1. **The Fable "harness" runs are missing passes.** `part3.log` lines 1–3: discovery returned 0 chars on both engines and inference 0 chars on argument_architecture; `outputs/conditions_of_possibility_analyzer__harness__fable.md:1-5` is an empty Pass 1 and `argument_architecture__harness__fable.md:1-13` is three empty passes; the model notices ("the shared context from Passes 1–3 arrived empty", line 17). The Fable harness is therefore a 3-pass and a 2-pass run that rebuilt its own foundations. That a crippled CoP harness still won 4/4 and a crippled AA harness lost 4/4 favours the engine-split reading over a model reading.
2. **No condition used extended thinking.** `engine_runner.py:47` defaults to sync and `:200-206` sets `effort=None` for Anthropic in sync mode; depth `deep` maps to the `opus` key which is `claude-sonnet-4-6` (`:51-58`, `:146-147`). "A strong model figuring it out" was tested without its thinking budget on both sides. Cost was not recorded (`manifest.json` `cost_usd: 0.0`); the proxies are time (Sonnet: 479s vs 77s, 499s vs 106s) and size (95K vs 14K, 100K vs 18K chars), roughly 6× the tokens (my estimate).

## 1. Does the harness beat the one-shot?

**On conditions_of_possibility, yes, and I agree with the fairness judge.** The best single analysis in the study is `conditions_of_possibility_analyzer__harness__fable.md` Pass 4 (lines 161–247), and its strongest claims check against the source: the paper's own definition of regulatory control as "motivated by geopolitics, which in turn reshapes market mechanisms" (source 377–379) contradicts its fetishism critique (176–178); the repair is latent in the paper's own vocabulary ("market-creating actions", 164; "reconstituting the market", 146; "constitution of market and the state", 43); the DPA Title III "domestic source" designation (421–422) is the paper's best fact and is never connected to the Cowen & Smith line it quotes (194–196); and Gina Rinehart sits in a cited headline (926–928) the text never mentions (Pass 3, line 125). The one-shot (`conditions_of_possibility_analyzer__oneshot__fable.md`) is very good at a quarter the length — the debts list (41–47), the "materiality test" (51), the "counterfactual journalist" (55) — but its headings are the dimension list and it reads as a checklist; the harness's "one hole, two faces" (Pass 4 §3) is a synthesis the one-shot did not reach. I disagree with the original judge on two counts: the concatenation, not the reading, lost (its complaints are "procedural self-narration", SUMMARY.md pairwise rows 2, 8), and its hallucination scoring penalises exactly what a Foucauldian/Skinnerian reading must do — bring the archive the text does not cite (van der Pijl, Chalmers Johnson, Weiss's *America Inc.*, Pass 2 lines 47–51). Those are unverifiable from the disk but they are expertise, not invention. A real risk does exist: Sonnet's Pass 1 places Jayasuriya at "Western Sydney University" (`__harness__sonnet.md:17`); the source's contributor notes (814–820) point to Murdoch.

**On argument_architecture, the one-shot is the better document, and I only half agree that it is the better reading.** `argument_architecture__oneshot__fable.md` is the cleanest Toulmin map in the study; its grounds (line 7) are all verifiable (USD 258m, 426; 19.1%, 534; 400 USD billion, 710; Sequoia ~USD 6m, 730–731; Eximbank 40%, 464; the Polish orders, fn 3, 807–810), its six suppressed premises (53–60) are the right six, and its "denominator-blindness" (27) is a real catch. But the harness's Pass 4 (`__harness__fable.md:101-169`) has the two sharpest findings on this engine — the warrant is *imported* from Wijaya & Jayasuriya 2024 so the paper is "an application masquerading as a demonstration" (107–115), and the text holds two theses, a strong displacement claim carried by the rhetoric and a weak supplementation claim carried by the evidence, with every qualifier retreating from the first to the second (131–141). The judge calls both "genuinely penetrating" and rejects the document as "addressed to a machine, not an executive" (`judgments_integration_only.json`, AA/Fable rows) — right for its criterion, the wrong criterion for a production pass (§4).

**Which mechanism did the work.** Not the probing questions on their own: `oneshot_questions` scored within a point of `oneshot` on every axis (SUMMARY.md rubric). The compounding came from pass-to-pass context plus the confrontation stance: Pass 3 of the CoP Fable run *argues with Pass 2* ("Correction one/two/three", lines 123–127) and produces the causal-grammar inversion and the Rinehart catch; Pass 4 then shows Pass 2's fault and Pass 3's fault are one (203–209). No one-shot did that on this text. Depth contributed volume (§2). The cost was coherence: word-counts of "pass" are 62 and 24 in the CoP harness files and 0 in every one-shot; time ×5–6; and, for AA, a final pass that ends with instructions to three other engines.

**The right criterion for passes 1–3** is not usefulness to an executive but *yield to the next reader*: did the pass surface what the final pass and the spine will need (the 24-hour timeline, 329; "wedged", 352–353; Title III; the Curran/White sources; the Rinehart headline), can it be cited (anchors), and does it add findings the prior pass lacked. Tracing Pass 4's claims to their originating pass makes this measurable; the fairness check measures only the last link.

## 2. Is the multi-pass structure the right structure?

**What passes 2 and 3 add.** On Sonnet, where all passes exist: CoP Pass 2 builds the "one column" load path and the nine-link chain (`__harness__sonnet.md:186-228`); Pass 3 finds the bipartisanship paradox (260–276), which the judge later names the sharpest critique in either CoP analysis; Pass 4 integrates. For AA, Pass 2's burden-shifting analysis (`argument_architecture__harness__sonnet.md:237-330`) is real work, but the judge's note that "the unfalsifiability point recurs three times with minimal new content" is fair: a mapping method has one object; re-mapping it under a new stance re-describes it.

**Stances** do work only where the method compounds. Confrontation buys self-correction; discovery buys the 37K-char "Layer 1…Layer 11" catalogue (`conditions_of_possibility_analyzer__harness__sonnet.md:3-120`) that no desk reads. Note that no stance text (`src/operations/definitions/stances.yaml`) asks for a verbatim anchor, and the only "cite textual evidence" line lives in the whole-engine path (`capability_composer.py:538-539`), which per-pass composition never uses (`:190`). The one-shot prompt says "Anchor every substantive claim in a short verbatim quote" (`scripts/study_engine_harness.py:76`). The judge's first criterion is anchoring. Much of the 12/12 is this asymmetry.

**consumes_from** carries whole prior prose (`context_broker.py:108-152`, no cap at inner level) under a header that says "do not repeat them" (`capability_composer.py:476-478`). The model obeys by narrating what prior passes did. Three composition defects sit here:

- `chain_runner.py:359-364` rebuilds `PassDefinition` with `description=""`, so the operationalization's per-pass description — the best-written instruction in the system, e.g. the 15-line confrontation brief at `src/operationalizations/definitions/conditions_of_possibility_analyzer.yaml:52-73` — never reaches the model. I composed the runtime prompts offline to confirm: the pass section is a bare label.
- `capability_composer.py:358-360` tells *every* pass, including the last, "Your output will be read by the next analytical pass".
- `:365-372` renders `composability.shares_with` lines when a key substring-matches a focus dimension. AA's keys equal its dimension keys (`argument_architecture.yaml:451-462`), so its final pass is told to surface material for `dialectical_structure_analyzer`, `specialized_reasoning_classifier` and `theory_construction_analyzer` — and duly ends with "For downstream engines" sections (`__harness__fable.md:163-169`; `__harness__sonnet.md:613`). CoP's keys (`actors_and_institutions`, `path_dependency_chains`…) match nothing, so its final pass is written for a reader. **This accident is a large part of why the engines split.**

**Depth** is a real dial for pass count (`depth_sequences`, op YAML 102–136) and one sentence per dimension (`capability_composer.py:429-431`). It changes neither model, nor max_tokens, nor thinking. "Deep" means "more passes and a request for exhaustiveness", whence the 37K discovery pass.

## 3. Definitions

**conditions_of_possibility_analyzer.** Eight dimensions (`yaml:124-235`), of which enabling_conditions, path_dependencies and unacknowledged_debts ask the same question three ways — the outputs list the same six citations under each — and alternative_paths is assigned to two passes (op YAML 44–47, 68–71). Load-bearing: constraining_conditions (it produced the hedge analysis), counterfactual_analysis (the quarter/two-thirds stratification, Pass 4 219–223), synthetic_judgment (the five costed repairs, 239), and cross_domain_transfers (the "Southeast Asian method transposed to the Anglosphere" finding, 193 — the most Foucauldian thing in the study). The probing questions are generic ("What frameworks from prior work are reused or presupposed?"); what Skinner would ask — what was the author *doing*, against whom, in which venue — the Fable Pass 4 answered unprompted (171–191). The engine was written for an author's corpus ("author with 3-5 prior works", `yaml:555`; "10+ works", `:608`; consumes from four corpus engines, `:513-522`) and ran here on one paper; the model reinterpreted "prior work" as the cited school. The model carried the method, not the definition. Lineage reaches the prompt as five tradition names and seven concept names (`capability_composer.py:400-408`); the `intellectual_grounding.method` and `indicators` blocks (`yaml:252-262`, e.g. "argument begins mid-conversation, presupposing resolution of debates the author previously engaged") — the material that would make the model *look* like Foucault — are not rendered at all.

**argument_architecture.** Six distinct, load-bearing dimensions (`yaml:149-267`) with questions an argumentation theorist would ask. That is why the one-shot suffices: the method is textbook and inside the model; the one-shot task paragraph already names it. What the lineage could add is Walton's critical questions *per scheme* — the prompt says "the critical questions specific to each scheme" without listing them; the models supplied the sign-scheme question themselves ("could the sign be produced by something else", `__harness__fable.md:123`).

**How lineage should enter:** as a method card, not biography — for the pass's `focus_capabilities` (carried at `capability_composer.py:308`, never rendered), the `method` sentence plus the `indicators`. No worked example is needed; the model knows the traditions and needs the cues to hunt.

## 4. Process and workflows

Engine prose is read by the spine (`spine.py:46, 218`, 60K chars/phase), the tables desk (`tables.py:92, 205`), the figures desk (`figures.py:127`) and compose (`compose.py:103`) — all through `analysis_prose` (`common.py:183-191`), all seeing only the last pass. Production therefore already does what the fairness check did, which is the good news: the 12/12 verdict was on a document no desk sees. The bad news is what that last pass lacks: the tables desk must anchor rows in the documents "not from the analysis prose" (`tables.py:57`), and the spine's `Anchor.quote` wants a verbatim phrase (`schemas.py:360-363`) — the engine's quotations are unverified and the spine re-finds them (DESIGN_concretization §B.4–B.5 documents what that produces). Prose is the right medium for reasoning and the wrong *only* medium for handoff. The fix consistent with the LLM-first doctrine is a model-declared findings ledger closing each pass — finding, verbatim anchor ≤200 chars, dimension, confidence — with code doing shape and verbatim-membership only (`walls.verify_anchor` exists). The spine reads ledgers plus prose; the study reads ledgers to trace which pass produced which final finding. First changes in the chain, in order: the final-pass instruction, the anchoring line, then the judge — two judges, a *yield* judge for intermediate passes and a *handoff* judge for the last, neither asked to brief an executive.

## 5. The set-up as a whole

273 legacy definitions, 28 executable (`catalog.py:200`): 15 synthesis, 9 extraction, 2 primitive, 1 relational, 1 comparison; 18 operationalized, every one at 3–4 passes at `deep` regardless of kind. The evidence says kind should decide: a relational/mapping method (AA) was better, five times cheaper and faster as one call with the engine's own questions; a synthesis/judgment method (CoP) needed the passes. The registry of 200-plus is not the unit; the unit is roughly a dozen methods (kind × function — genealogy 14, logic 10, influence 4) with parameters (dimensions, questions, method card, pass count). I would invest in that smaller set with better definitions and a fixed harness, run mapping engines one-shot, and not spend on the long tail. I would not go pure one-shot: the CoP result and the Pass 3 self-corrections are things the one-shots did not produce here. Two cautions from running the study: Fable is not production-ready in this harness (forced tool use rejected; instant empty responses on discovery/inference, `part2.log`, `part3.log:1-3`, with `stop_reason` not logged — speculation: a request-shape or refusal issue, not generation); and the study is one source, two engines, one judge model, no thinking.

## 6. Proposals, ranked

1. **Fix the final-pass prompt.** *Where:* `capability_composer.py:353-379`; `chain_runner.py:359-367`. *What:* on the last pass, replace "read by the next analytical pass" with a desk-facing instruction (anchored, no process narration, no addresses to other engines) and drop the `shares_with` tail; add "anchor every substantive claim in a short verbatim quote" to every pass; pass the operationalization description through instead of `""`. *Why:* judge reasons on AA name exactly these tails; the CoP/AA split traces to `:365-372`. *Test:* rerun the AA and CoP integration-only pairings (8 judge calls, 4 engine passes); expect AA to close and CoP not to regress.
2. **Route by kind.** *Where:* operationalization schema + `chain_runner._run_engine_passes` (`:256-283` already has the single-call fallback). *What:* `execution_mode: oneshot_questions | passes`, defaulting from `kind`; let `deep` also set `effort` so depth means something (`engine_runner.py:205-206`). *Why:* AA one-shot 4/4 at ~1/5 the time; CoP passes 4/4. *Test:* one more relational and one more synthesis engine on this source in both modes; adopt if the split holds by kind.
3. **Findings ledger per pass.** *Where:* pass instructions + `output_store` + `spine.py:218`. *What:* model-declared findings with verbatim anchors, wall-checked, carried in inner-pass context and read by the spine. *Why:* §4; gives the study its intermediate-pass criterion. *Test:* anchors surviving the wall per pass; Pass-4 findings traced to origin pass; spine `buried_crux` with and without.
4. **Render the method card.** *Where:* `compose_pass_prompt` (`:171-178`), using `focus_capabilities`. *What:* `intellectual_grounding.method` + `indicators` for the pass's capabilities; prune CoP's overlapping dimensions to five. *Why:* lineage is currently five words; the indicators are the operative Foucault/Skinner cues. *Test:* compose offline to confirm inclusion, then CoP Pass 1 with/without, judged for yield.
5. **Instrument empty responses.** *Where:* `src/dossier/llm.py:_run`, the Anthropic backend. *What:* log `stop_reason`/usage and raise on empty content. *Why:* three silent empties shaped this study. *Test:* rerun the discovery prompt on Fable five times.
