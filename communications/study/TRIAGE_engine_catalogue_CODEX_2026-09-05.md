## 6. Shared reading duties to preserve during consolidation

The saved Codex family file supplies imperative critic duties and shared method cards for each family. The following are the recurring requirements most directly connected to the demand gaps and the study failures. They are not method-count quotas.

- Attribute each claim and each compared side to its source and speaker; distinguish reported positions from endorsement and independent verification.
- Align meaning, scope, quantifiers, dates and modal force before alleging agreement, contradiction, revision or corroboration.
- Match warrants to the actual claim and genre; distinguish conceptual, constitutive, interpretive, normative and empirical questions.
- Reconstruct the strongest faithful argument and preserve independent support routes before removing a premise or proposing a challenge.
- Require evidence for each relation, including its endpoints, direction and type; keep causal production, logical implication, dependence and co-occurrence separate.
- Establish an expectation, comparison baseline and inspected coverage before reporting absence, an anomaly, a trend, a cycle or a literature gap.
- Preserve units, denominators, populations, dates and target-versus-observation status, and test source independence before counting corroboration.
- Separate observed change, source explanation, analyst hypothesis and conditional projection; stop where evidence ends and allow an unresolved or negative result.
- Reject unsupported motive, awareness, concealment, audience-effect and career claims while preserving source-attested reports about those matters as reports.
- Carry evidence limits, check status, source roles and scoped negative outcomes into the commissioned artifact; do not manufacture findings to fill a promised row or figure count.
