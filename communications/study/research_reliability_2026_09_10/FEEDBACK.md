# Experimental source-reading feedback

`research_evidence_feedback@1` is a centrally registered, executable Analyst method. Its frozen capability, operationalization, four source/causal/concept/institutional criticism dependencies and hashes travel with each call. `GET /v1/engines/research_evidence_feedback/method?version=1` retrieves it. The existing light-call endpoint dispatches this method through a bounded execution path; it returns validated observations, one search-or-stop decision and a charge/reservation receipt.

The method reviews acquired original windows, revises competing explanations and names a discriminating next observation. Reporter executes its focused follow-up under the original source population and date policy. Stacks owns the durable request reservation, acquisition identities, frozen snapshots, round limit and separate discovery/analysis allowances. Stacks cannot adjudicate the intellectual relevance of a query. Missing/ambiguous support, repeated focuses or an unavailable response cause a recorded stop; the final inquiry can continue with existing originals. Lost responses retain their complete invocation allowance and are not automatically retried.

The method has a 6,000-token output ceiling, applied before provider reservations and carried through retries/fallbacks. Its composition follows the decision contract rather than mandatory document-order exposition. Other methods retain their existing composition. Research packets are labeled research context rather than citation-explainer packets. The light-call adapter now consumes the same quote-aware fields as the wall.

The experiment remains off by default. Stacks exposes one or two feedback rounds for a new automatic Reporter search. Initial discovery and continuations divide the displayed discovery cap. Each review reserves the smaller of $2 and 20% of the displayed analysis budget. Verified/conservatively reserved usage is subtracted before the final Analyst submission. The remaining final-analysis allowance is never borrowed for extra discovery. Institutional inquiries retain their institution-only policy. Sources and preliminary observations remain explicitly distinct from the final memo's independent source readings.

Selection recovery also preserves contradictory rows that reuse a ledger ID. A batch conflict omitted from global reconciliation remains a conflict requiring explicit resolution. Completed historical investigations return their stored answer unchanged; a legacy synthesis whose supplied evidence identities differ from the corrected route cannot be silently reused.

Validation: central method composition, actual engine/backend-boundary token reservations using a fake provider, quote/identity checks, conflict recovery, method freezing, resumption and investigation suites passed without paid APIs. Stacks tests cover lost-response reservations, immutable snapshot lineage, source-window offsets, stop/no-new-source behavior, and subtraction of review usage before final submission. Browser checks cover explicit opt-in and the visible decision trail.

An expanded unrelated check of `tests/test_phase3_prompt_budget.py` found four failures (three source-corpus assembly expectations and a removed budget helper). The same four failures reproduce on the untouched `888fac42` baseline. That chain is outside this change.

No regenerated-answer comparison has run. Promotion requires the independent audit's cost-bounded Tether and held-out-case evaluation, not a count of queries, plans, or recovered citations. Trial 1 (same-evidence routing, up to $8) remains the first proposed separately authorized paid comparison. Do not use historical unused allowances. Raw original bodies and model outputs stay in private artifacts; release receipts contain hashes and aggregate checks only.
