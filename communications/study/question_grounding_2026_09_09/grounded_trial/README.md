# Frozen-primary question-development trial — 9 September 2026

The revised method produced a source-grounded question that makes a consequential correction to the original framing. Riley and Brenner already allow a historical role for state-directed productive growth. The result therefore asks how different growth arrangements form conflicting political interests and affect international solidarity, rather than treating any politically organized growth as a refutation of their account.

This is one isolated scholarly trial, following the [controlled discovery comparison](../README.md) and its [written brief](../../../CODEX_PROMPT_question_grounding_2026-09-09.md). It supplies evidence that the revised method can develop this question from actual primary texts. It does not establish the best interpretation of Riley, the author's endorsement of the proposed question, or successful production automation.

## Result and substantive assessment

The [validated result](development/validated_result.json) proposes:

> If politically organized accumulation sustains productive growth, how would its uneven gains and costs shape whether workers join national growth coalitions, and what would Riley's account of class formation need to explain about building international solidarity under those conditions?

The result preserves the original links among capitalist renewal, class politics and international solidarity. It makes national growth coalitions an explicit proposed mechanism and keeps productive renewal conditional. It also offers an accumulation-first alternative, distinguishing interventions that expand productive capacity from those that inflate asset claims, rather than silently deciding that political consequences must be the author's priority.

The most consequential evidence is in the Riley/Brenner reply to critics: wartime state investment, together with capital destruction, figures in their account of recovery. Inspection of the surrounding paragraph confirms that this is their argument, not merely a position they quote from someone else. The result uses that passage as counterevidence to a blanket incompatibility between political intervention and growth. A separate passage connecting Long Boom growth to redistributive compromise identifies a dependency to investigate; it does not establish that growth produces solidarity.

The result also preserves Riley's distinction between economic and class interests and the constitutive role of struggle in forming interests. It explicitly carries forward the actual second investigation's correction of the first investigation: durable organization, solidarity and an alternative order can be achievements of class formation rather than necessary prior conditions. Those earlier analyses remain secondary context. The proposed connection between historical geopolitics and contemporary solidarity remains a hypothesis.

Its suggested constructive inquiry refers to the actual approved commitments `part:1` and `part:6`. It is a proposed handoff, not an executed inquiry or accepted belief. The author still needs to judge whether the political-interest question or the accumulation-first alternative better serves the project.

## Limits and next acceptance step

The earlier discovery comparison isolates a method change from a context change more directly than this trial. This continuation demonstrates one feasible, grounded result after the revised discovery decision. It has no repeated runs, blinded scholarly rating or old-method grounded-development counterpart. It cannot establish a general quality improvement or causal effect on downstream scholarly outcomes.

The source pool came from an already completed investigation. This trial therefore does not test whether the deployed workflow discovers that corpus, acquires missing texts, resumes safely after delays, or displays and executes the next activity correctly. The next meaningful production acceptance case is the same original question with its held material made visible automatically, followed by the author's real assessment of the proposed question and one consequential follow-on activity. It should retain the source coverage, uncertainty and competing priorities visible here.
