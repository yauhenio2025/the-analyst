## What the readers found

The production questions make the papers' claims and dependencies easier to inspect. They also sometimes require a stronger diagnosis than the evidence supports. A philosophical paper can interpret a method without using that method itself; a premise used to establish a conclusion need not follow from accepting the conclusion; a repeated position can be argued again. These distinctions explain several concrete errors across the three papers and the completed corpus readings.

The mechanical wall and the critic serve different purposes. A quotation can occur in a document while supporting another speaker, a qualified claim, or a different inferential role. The study exposes both deterministic defects in carrying quotations and rulings forward, and substantive mistakes that survive perfectly valid quotation matches. Neither the verified-row rate nor a confident critic ruling is an accuracy estimate.

Harris is a useful test of inferential necessity. His eight-argument case adopts a transformative criterion for critical theory, but separate fatal objections can support abandonment of recognition theory. The claim that monism is constitutive therefore cannot automatically become a premise every reader must accept. His explicit optionality about Thompson likewise prevents a list of possible successor resources from becoming a necessary theoretical programme.

Zambrana tests both attribution and evidential roles. She adopts Rose's method while rejecting Rose's reading of Marx, then uses historical scholarship to support slavery's internality to capitalism. That combination is neither automatically inconsistent nor established by accepting speculative vocabulary alone. Primary textual interpretation and historical evidence have different jobs in the argument. The checked Conditions finding that historical support comes entirely from a single lecture is false: the paper also uses Smallwood's book.

Chen tests conditions and warrant. His positive proposal retains the scientific/social analogy conditionally, and criticism of Jaeggi can survive failure of that proposal. Reasons and standards also survive rejection of a neutral criterion. The bridge from scientific community judgment to social majority judgment and collective constitutivity still needs scrutiny. Claims about what people actually believe and claims about what recognition constitutes cannot be tested by silently substituting one evidential burden for the other.

These assessments were written from the actual outputs and supplied sources before Sonnet's judgments. They are not inferred from model preferences.

## Interpretation and next work

This compares the complete production treatment—questions, method cards, output brief and check—with original-question one-call readings. It does not isolate the contribution of questions or criticism. Each case is one stochastic reading per condition, across three selected philosophy papers. Sonnet sees opposite A/B orders; only agreement in both counts. Formatting and the Check receipt can reveal the treatment despite the labels. Corpus assessments are source-based reader judgments without a pairwise baseline.

The direct corpus runner receives one keyed entry per document. The application's workflow adapter still wraps its incoming text as one document, so this study does not certify application corpus dispatch. That documented exposure prerequisite remains open.
