# Inferential Commitment Mapper: three-paper evaluation, 2026-09-05

The checked mapper makes commitments, attribution, and dependencies easier to inspect, but does not consistently improve inferential necessity. Its strongest result is Chen: the alternative remains conditional, reasons survive rejection of a neutral criterion, and failure of the constructive argument need not destroy the critique. Harris and Zambrana expose a recurrent error: premises used to establish a conclusion become obligations supposedly incurred merely by accepting that conclusion. The old readings already capture much of the substantive argument. This is an independent source-based assessment, not a Sonnet verdict; pairwise judgments are pending here.

Both Harris readings substantially recover this package. Old F8–22 already distinguish mechanisms, experiences, constitutive power, colonial domination, and institutional repair. Checked C1–C11 make the architecture more explicit, including degrees of defense. Neither text licenses treating every cited thinker as mandatory: accepting the colonial criticism does not itself endorse every remedy in Fanon quotations.

Thompson provides the clearest necessity test. Harris expressly writes, “Whether one agrees with Thompson’s approach, or not” (775–792). Checked F25 correctly treats a replacement theory as unconstructed; the old prose also says Harris does not require Thompson’s particular alternative. Nevertheless, checked consequences insist foundations “must incorporate” a list including psychoanalysis and social ontology. Harris’s examples of available resources do not establish each as indispensable. The old “construct alternative foundations using” list has a similar tendency, despite its qualification. The necessary explanatory and transformative capacities should be separated from plausible successor resources.

Checked Harris also overstates dependency. Its opening makes accepting abandonment require monism’s constitutive status, while F21 says correctable defects warrant abandonment “only if monism is constitutive.” That reconstructs one important route as a necessary route. Section 3.4 attacks recognition’s primacy “regardless of whether other approaches are also used” (567–578); note 3 attacks even rescoped adequacy (828–834). A reader could accept abandonment through another fatal objection while rejecting the identity premise. Old F26 more usefully isolates the disputed claim of structural inaccessibility, although it too could distinguish alternative routes more carefully.

The critic’s rejection of checked F20 illustrates both value and limitation. It rejects an alleged conflict between recognition’s residual usefulness and the framework’s inability to study any pathology. That rejection can be defensible: a minimal recognition concept inside another theory differs from Honneth’s framework, and the first passage is conditional. But the supplied reason wrongly calls it merely Herranen and Laitinen’s view. Lines 353–371 contain Harris’s own counterfactual discussion. Worse, the final prose and open question 5 still assert the rejected internal conflict. Updating the ledger has not reconciled the reading.

**Zambrana** is a successful attribution test. Both outputs understand adopting Rose’s speculative method while rejecting Rose’s interpretation of Marx. Old F17/F23 capture the productive extension beyond Marx’s restricted application; checked F10–11 preserve this distinction explicitly. This is precisely what the [source](../../data/study/sources_ideas/zambrana2025_philosophy_in_the_severe_style_rose.txt), lines 67–95 and 313–321, requires. There is no evidence here for adding another generic productive-tension question.

There is evidence for changing its representation. Checked F12 says apparent autonomy “excludes literal independence,” then explains that autonomy is a real appearance compatible with material dependence. F13 places nonteleology against necessity but correctly explains necessity through contingent conditions. The current I3 question already asks which tension is productive; its answer shape nevertheless requires “A excludes B.” These rows need relations that distinguish contradiction, qualified appearance, methodological tension, and attribution to an opponent. Otherwise accurate explanations sit beneath misleading relation labels.

Dependency again slips in the opening: accepting C1, that Marx practices speculative thinking, supposedly commits the reader to slavery’s constitutive place in capitalism. Checked F17 itself supplies the missing qualification: Smallwood’s historical claim is an additional premise. Speculative vocabulary cannot establish which historical relations generated capitalist forms. The old discussion better separates this historical bridge, although old F26 also overstates the damage from rejecting actuality. Checked F15’s `failure-takes` remains broad; its final judgment is better calibrated: historical evidence may remain compelling while the “specifically speculative unity” fails. Checked F19–21 also preserve the source’s tentative enlargement of action without inventing a program; old prose goes too far in forbidding wage labor as even a primary site of action. The critic’s concrete F9 rewrite narrows its finding toward the historical reach of mystification, but does not repair the opening’s broader dependency claim.

**Chen** shows the clearest improvement in conditional scope. The [source](../../data/study/sources_ideas/chen2025_progress_without_progress_jaeggi.txt), lines 565–580, argues for the psychologist alternative if scientific/social parallelism is retained. Checked I1.F7 preserves “if we insist”; counter-evidence explicitly leaves rejecting the analogy available. Old F1 and its opening make replacement sound less conditional. Checked I4.F24 also correctly limits failure of collective constitutivity to the positive alternative: “the critique of Jaeggi may survive.” This is the dependency discipline missing in Harris.

Both Chen readings retain reasons and standards: old F21 and checked I3.F19 resist equating absence of a neutral criterion with irrationalism. Both distinguish collective judgment constituting progress from merely reporting belief about it; checked I5.F27 usefully exposes the unspecified collective, legitimacy, and dissent burden. The source gives the know-how reply a hearing and answers the populism objection; those are qualified replies, not unnoticed problems. Checked prose is correspondingly careful. A residual attribution issue remains in I5.F25: its proposed social research consequence is anchored in Kuhn’s instruction about scientific communities. Applying that instruction to society should be marked as an inferred extension, not simply a drawn consequence.

The priorities are therefore narrow: make I2 distinguish accepting a conclusion from accepting its complete derivation, naming additional premises; make I4 preserve independent routes and scope exactly what loses support, relaxing its demand for a single point of failure; and allow I3 relation types with explicit claim owners and qualifications. For I5, separate necessary capacities, advocated resources, and analyst-proposed extensions. Finally, propagate rejected or weakened findings into dependent prose. These changes answer observed failures while preserving the mapper’s demonstrated strengths: explicit commitment inventories, selective attribution, conditional conclusions, and visible unresolved burdens.
