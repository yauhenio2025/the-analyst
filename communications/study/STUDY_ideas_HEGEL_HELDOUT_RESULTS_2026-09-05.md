## What the source review establishes

The changes produce concrete improvements in the intended areas. They do not eliminate the recurring errors. The previous definitions already made several of the right distinctions; the comparison must credit actual gains beyond that baseline.

| Engine | Substantive improvement | Remaining limit |
|---|---|---|
| Conditions | More precise premise removal and independently surviving support; clearer distinction between assumed initial unity and unity earned by a derivation. | Revised Elling's opening still removes every reason to defend habit when one institutional premise is withdrawn, though its later analysis preserves independent support. Revised Ganzinger calls performative contradiction uncited despite the explicit Martin citation. |
| Argument | Explicit objectivity/content branches in Ganzinger and three partially independent routes in Elling. | Claim/inference labels still change meaning between prose and ledger. Revised Elling calls an expressly discussed disanalogy unaddressed. The revised Ganzinger critic replaces a conceptual-warrant question with lack of empirical evidence. |
| Commitment | Explicit permission to accept a conclusion without its whole derivation; named extra premises and surviving branches. | Revised Ganzinger's opening and closing still sometimes impose the derivation as the price of conclusion acceptance. Proposed intellectual intuition and self-produced conceptual activity need finer distinctions. |
| Epistemology | Studied, advocated and enacted methods are separated. Selective adoption and substantive qualifications receive clearer treatment. Revised Elling fixes the false mismatch between weak criticism and necessary threat. | Some comparisons still treat a tentative taxonomy and a necessary conclusion as the same claim changing strength. Conceptual conditional arguments sometimes inherit an empirical burden; local reconstructed enactment must remain possible. |

Three lessons recur across the pairs. First, compare the **same proposition, scope and modality**: actual free habit is compatible with necessary vulnerability, and risk reduction does not alone defeat residual risk. Second, distinguish an alternative that is missing from one that is discussed without a sufficient answer. Third, assess conceptual, interpretive and empirical warrants on their own terms. A source need not use an analyst's label such as “greatest burden” for a grounded analytical reconstruction to be legitimate.

The check itself needs scrutiny. In revised Commitment/Ganzinger it calls a genuine introduction quotation a misquotation because the abstract uses a different, also genuine wording. In previous Commitment/Elling it claims to merge a rejected finding into another row without carrying the full proposition into that row. Quotation membership, full ruling coverage and judge preference cannot certify these interpretations. The checked product's introductory prose remains the original reading, so a changed ledger may also leave its summary inconsistent.
