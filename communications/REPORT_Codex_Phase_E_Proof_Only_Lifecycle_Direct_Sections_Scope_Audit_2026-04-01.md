**Recommendation**

Approve the scope after four corrections.

- Reframe the slice as a host-boundary generality proof over an already-proved lifecycle seam, not as the first proof that analyzer-owned save/reopen exists.
- Replace the `source_selection` claim with the more precise contract statement: the current public save seam does not directly admit it, and honest support would need either request-schema widening or a new analyzer-owned lowered-request/save seam.
- Tighten the acceptance bar to the standalone-harness reality: fresh-navigation reopen, one `GET /compose-sessions/{session_id}`, zero second compose call, zero hidden in-memory fallback, and exact saved-session fidelity on `consumer_key`, `workflow_key`, `presentation_hash`, `presentation_content_hash`, and `resolver_version`.
- Add one harness-specific identity correction: `session_id` must be surfaced as lifecycle identity and `planning_decision_id` must remain provenance only.

No other next step should come first. A broader `source_selection` lifecycle slice would be larger and less honest under the current public contract, while another consumer-identity or shell-broadening slice would just retest a variable that was already closed on April 1.
