**What the Mastermind is asked to hold**: the repertoire of lane kinds as a vocabulary record (owner the-stacks — mechanism · genealogy / history ·
reception · distinction · alternative interpretation · paradigm · periodisation · criterion · counterexample · objection · stakes, to be added to
or struck by him); the practices of filing an arrival into lanes and of re-deriving a logical outline from lanes and materials; and the knowledge,
in the existing engines (`interlocutor_position`, `distinction_*`, `encounter_*`, `reference_reread`, `hypothesis_evidential_frame`), that they
run *inside* a lane. The Stacks' examination of its own code and page for this is the commission
`zotero-stacks/communications/COMMISSION_FOUR_DIMENSIONS_EXAMINATION_2026-09-11.md`; a note for this repo's session follows from its plan.
