# Task for the zotero-stacks session: the cohort citation flow, built on the Mastermind's engines (6 Sep 2026)

## The brief, in the owner's words (dictated, lightly cleaned)

All our citation-universe operations presume one-to-one correspondence: how Dylan Riley engages Weber, how he engages Marx. Now I want to set up a cohort of thinkers (not to confuse with our bundles) and test one thinker against all of them. Say I want to see how Brenner has engaged with the regulation school. I write "Brenner and the regulation school" and an LLM-powered flow goes and systematically finds the regulation-school people from its knowledge, puts them up, looks up citations for each, adds the analysis steps it needs (how he cites each of them), and at the end produces an overall memo. At the beginning a smart model does the planning: Fable strategizes how to structure the final memo. It has to be agentic. And it should work with the engines that live inside the Mastermind from the start, because that is where the engines will live; do not build a one-to-many extension on the old lane and port it later.

## What exists now, on both sides

**Mastermind (the-analyst, master as of this evening):**
- Three citation engines, built from the Stacks' own prompts and validated on a Riley → Weber corpus tonight: `citation_engagement_map` (A on P over A's citing texts: place of P per text, per-passage move and stance with anchors, a trajectory across A's work), `citation_fidelity_audit` (pairs of A's passage and P's cited page: attribution kind, what A attributes, what P says with a verbatim anchor in P, verdict accurate · fair · selective · stretched · misattributed · unverifiable, with `how: page | section | search`), `citation_reception_map` (held readers of P: argument, stance, position per theme, with the plan's themes as spine and the readers' themes as labelled additions; where A sits among readers). Modes: surface oneshot, standard oneshot_checked (one read + a critic + rulings applied), deep dvs (parallel extraction per document, critic per document, synthesis). Release state and reports: `communications/study/STUDY_citation_family_2026-09-06.md`.
- The bridge contract (`communications/INTEGRATION_stacks_citation_universe_2026-09-06.md`, `REPLY_mastermind_to_stacks_2026-09-06.md`, and the Stacks' reply): the Stacks pushes; `POST /v1/dossier/jobs` with `sources` of kind `stacks_export` carrying the export inline (auto-split on the `===== [n/N] … [Library · Key] =====` headers, keyed by Zotero key), plus one source with `role: evidence_index` (the ledger's passages with loci and the retrieved page windows of the cited works; shape as in the Stacks' reply §6.3) and one with `role: plan` (Fable's plan: questions, warnings, themes). Context documents are not profiled and not counted in the corpus; every engine receives them as upstream context; the fidelity audit unpacks the index into its pairs (`src/sources/citation_evidence.py`). `entry: chosen` with a `path` of one or more engines; poll `GET /v1/dossier/jobs/{id}`; the dossier markdown, tables and receipts come back; a single engine can be asked for alone.
- Live desk: https://the-analyst-kcuc.onrender.com (health reports the deployed commit). No pull from Render to the Stacks; the Stacks pushes.

**Stacks:** the citation ledger (who cites whom, how often, where, with the first citing sentence and the printed page), the materials of a pair (held, wanted, fetched), the memo lane (the fast path for one pair; keep it), `GET /api/cites/evidence` and `markers=1` on the export (both announced for this week by the citation-memo session), the assembler (a brief → a bundle from 20k works), filing a returned dossier as a meta record with `stacks-engine: mastermind dossier-<id>`.

## The flow to build (Stacks orchestration; Mastermind analysis)

1. **Plan (Fable).** From "Brenner and the regulation school": the memo's purpose, its sections in order, the questions each must answer, the warnings (editions, translations, nested attributions, the difference between citing a school and citing a person), the themes. Output: the plan object the bridge carries as `role: plan`. Stacks-side, cached per cohort request.
2. **Expand the cohort (a strong model, then the ledger).** The model names the members of the school from its knowledge (with dates and one-line reasons); the ledger says which of them A actually cites or names, how often, in which texts, and which of their works. Members A never cites are kept in the list as "not engaged" (that absence is a finding). The Stacks owns this step; the ledger keeps the model honest.
3. **Materials per member.** A's citing texts for that member; the member's cited works held or wanted (fire the fetch when the cohort is confirmed); the reception texts for the member where the reception lens is wanted. The existing materials lane, run per pair, deduplicated across the cohort (A's texts are shared).
4. **Per-pair analysis (Mastermind).** One dossier job per pair with a chosen path: the engagement map always; the fidelity audit when the cited works are held; the reception map when asked. The evidence index and the plan go along as context documents. Poll, then file each result beside the pair's own memo if one exists.
5. **The cohort memo (Mastermind, then the Stacks' essay if wanted).** One final job over all pairs' outputs with a cohort dimension the Mastermind side is adding to the engagement map (A's uses across the members: whom he builds on, whom he uses as foil or authority, whom he never engages, how the engagement shifts by period). Until that dimension lands, the final memo is the Stacks' essay-over-tables desk over the per-pair ledgers; the pilot compares the two.
6. **Surfaces.** A cohort page under the universe (the members with engaged / not engaged, counts, the per-pair memos, the cohort memo); the pile gets the notices; costs shown before the button, as the universe does.

## Rules

- Both ways before anything is retired: the first cohort (Brenner and the regulation school) is written by this flow and, for two of its pairs, by the old memo lane; the owner reads both; then Sonnet in both orders.
- Every quote verified by code on both sides; the critic judges readings, the wall decides citability; a row whose anchor is not re-found is uncitable whatever the critic says.
- Costs: a pair through the engagement map at deep is about $5, the fidelity audit on an index about $2, the reception map about $6 at deep; a cohort of ten members is therefore a $30–80 run and needs the estimate on the button and a cap per cohort. Use standard depth by default and deep only where the plan asks.
- Do not extend `cites_memo.py` for one-to-many. Do not expose the Stacks to the internet for this; the Stacks pushes.
- Coordinate with the Mastermind session on the same machine for: the cohort dimension of the engagement map, the `role: profile` source (the Stacks' work profiles as a starting point instead of re-reading), and the fidelity check of the final memo against its sources (the Stacks' `digest_check`, which the Mastermind is folding into the fidelity audit as a second input).
