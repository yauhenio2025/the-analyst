## G1. Must the structures that coordinate millions also forget them — and if infrastructure can remember, who inherits what it learns?
*concept:* the relation between coordination and memory in shared structures — whether what passes through infrastructure can accumulate and be returned.
*overspecified:* Could a coordination device also be a memory — a traffic light that learns from everyone who passes through it and hands the learning back as common property — and why do we only ever build infrastructure that forgets?
*tension (HF002 × HF025):* silent coordination devices vs. know-how that dies privately.
*rubric:* asserts or contests (1) whether coordination structures retain/return what users know; (2) who owns or inherits infrastructure-borne learning; (3) forgetting or enclosure as a design choice rather than a natural fact.

## G2. What would an economy be for beings whose ends are products of their acting rather than inputs to it?
*concept:* the model of the wanting subject presupposed by economic coordination — wants as given inputs vs. outputs of action.
*overspecified:* …and if the calculation debate assumed fixed wants awaiting efficient satisfaction, was the whole century arguing about a creature that doesn't exist?
*tension (HF006 × HF001):* ends discovered in acting vs. a debate premised on given wants.
*rubric:* asserts or contests (1) the given-wants premise anywhere in economic thought; (2) coordination or allocation under endogenous, action-formed preferences; (3) what the calculation controversy presupposed about persons.

## G3. What is actually owed, and in which direction, when the means a people must rent were built from what was taken from them?
*concept:* the identity between the expropriated estate and the rented means — dependence as rent charged on one's own inheritance.
*overspecified:* If the machines the South must rent were built from labor and ore taken from the South, is every license fee a rent charged on one's own inheritance — and what would it mean to reclaim an estate that now runs in someone else's cloud?
*tension (HF024 × HF021):* frozen dead labor vs. dependence enforced through licensing.
*rubric:* asserts or contests (1) the provenance of rented/licensed means in prior extraction; (2) restitution, audit, or reclamation of technologically embodied inheritance; (3) the direction of debt in technological dependence.

## G4. Can being provided for be freedom — and what changes about the answer when provision loses the power to be withdrawn?
*concept:* the fulfilled form of provided-for freedom — whether the objection to delivered life is the delivery or the revocability.
*overspecified:* The subscription's real promise is a life relieved of maintenance — could that promise be kept by something that cannot be cancelled or repossessed, and would we still recognize freedom when it arrives as a quiet unbranded utility?
*tension (HF008 × HF023):* comfort-as-dependence vs. promises worth keeping.
*rubric:* asserts or contests (1) revocability/repossession as the crux of service-dependence; (2) unconditional or uncancellable provision as an institutional form; (3) whether maintenance-relief is itself an emancipatory good.

## G5. Must whatever defeats the bargain be a better bargain — and what becomes of an alternative that wins by the measure it opposed?
*concept:* the measure by which alternatives must win — victory by the incumbent's metric as a conversion of the victor.
*overspecified:* If the only thing that beats a bargain is a better bargain, can solidarity ever compete on price — and if it wins that way, does it survive its own victory, or must it offer something no receipt can show?
*tension (HF032 × HF013):* cheapness dissolving solidarity vs. selling as self-justification.
*rubric:* asserts or contests (1) price/convenience as the compulsory arena for alternatives; (2) commercial success transforming or corrupting solidaristic forms; (3) goods offered that are constitutively non-receiptable.

## G6. What part of the market actually produces the new — and does that part need the rest?
*concept:* the attribution of novelty within the market bundle — discovery as a detachable function vs. property of the whole.
*overspecified:* If we take the market apart, which piece actually produces the new — and once that small engine is isolated, does it still need the rivalry, the prices, the whole surrounding machinery, or has the machinery been taking credit for it?
*tension (HF003 × HF004):* the market as separable functions vs. novelty credited to the whole.
*rubric:* asserts or contests (1) which specific market function generates discovery; (2) whether that function operates without rivalry/prices elsewhere; (3) misattribution of novelty to the ensemble.

## G7. What becomes of critique under an order that grows by absorbing it — and what, if anything, cannot be digested?
*concept:* the fate of critique inside a critique-metabolizing system — reflexivity of the questioning itself.
*overspecified:* If the system remakes itself around every critique aimed at it, is each of our questions already raw material for its next version — and what would a question have to look like for the system to be unable to digest it?
*tension (HF012 × HF001):* mutation-through-absorption vs. doubt about the questions themselves.
*rubric:* asserts or contests (1) recuperation/absorption of opposition as a growth mechanism; (2) criteria for indigestible demands or questions; (3) the historical fate of specific critiques after absorption.

## G8. How much of what is sold as solving was already solved — and what exactly is enclosed when a service outlives the unrecorded knowledge it replaced?
*concept:* enclosure by outliving — commodified problem-management feeding on the death of vernacular solutions.
*overspecified:* How many problems now managed by subscription were already quietly solved in kitchens and workshops — and what exactly is sold when an app replaces a fix the neighbors knew, and the knowing then dies out with them?
*tension (HF014 × HF025):* problems farmed as revenue vs. know-how perishing unrecorded.
*rubric:* asserts or contests (1) pre-existing vernacular solutions to now-serviced problems; (2) business models dependent on the extinction of free alternatives; (3) what is actually transacted when service replaces knowledge.

## G9. What could coordinate those still discovering their destinations — and would such a thing still be infrastructure, or something we have no name for?
*concept:* coordination under indeterminate ends — devices for agents whose destinations form in the moving.
*overspecified:* The traffic light coordinates people who already know where they're going — what could coordinate people still discovering where they want to go…
*tension (HF002 × HF006):* end-given coordination vs. end-discovering persons.
*rubric:* asserts or contests (1) the fixed-destination assumption inside any coordination mechanism; (2) forms that coordinate exploration rather than execution; (3) whether "infrastructure" survives as a category for open-ended coordination.

## G10. Which promises survive every version of the system unkept — and is constant unkeepability the surest sign of what only something else could honor?
*concept:* invariance of unkept promises across the system's self-transformations — molting as a sieve for what to rescue.
*overspecified:* As the system sheds skin after skin, which promises survive every molting unkept — and are those precisely the ones worth rescuing, because no version of it can honor them and something else entirely would have to?
*tension (HF023 × HF012):* promises worth rescuing vs. a shape-shifting promisor.
*rubric:* asserts or contests (1) a promise's persistence across named regimes/epochs of capitalism; (2) constitutive (not contingent) unkeepability; (3) trans-systemic redemption as the criterion of rescue-worthiness.

## G11. Does dependence have one grammar across its scales — and can an escape invented at one scale be translated to another?
*concept:* the scale-invariance of the dependence relation — household subscription and national licensing as one structure at two magnifications.
*overspecified:* If the tenant who owns nothing and the nation that licenses everything are caught in the same relation at different scales, could an escape invented at one scale be translated to the other — and which end of the ladder cracks first?
*tension (HF008 × HF021):* dependence by subscription vs. dependence by license.
*rubric:* asserts or contests (1) structural identity of dependence across scales; (2) portability of exit strategies between scales; (3) which scale is the weaker link and why.

## G12. How much of the new is the unacknowledged dead — and what changes when invention is treated as inheritance-work, owed backward as much as sold forward?
*concept:* the debt structure of novelty — innovation as standing on an unpaid estate of frozen skill.
*overspecified:* If every innovation stands on a vast unpaid estate of frozen skill, is the new mostly the old wearing a founder's face — and what changes when a society treats invention as inheritance-work, owed backward as much as sold forward?
*tension (HF004 × HF024):* the cult of the new vs. the frozen estate of the dead.
*rubric:* asserts or contests (1) the composition of innovation out of prior accumulated skill; (2) attribution and its economics (the founder's face); (3) institutional forms of backward-owed invention.

## What the whole set is pregnant with (round-1 read, unchanged)

Taken together, the fourteen megas are relentlessly diagnostic: they name dependences, decompositions, promises, and inheritances, but never ask the constructive question their diagnoses make urgent — what would actually be built, by whom, and how one gets from the naming to the building. They are also pregnant with a missing subject: every question presumes a 'we' capable of rescuing, unbundling, reclaiming, or withstanding, yet no question asks who that we is, how it forms, or whether the same forces being diagnosed have already dissolved it. The set circles the door of transition without ever asking what turning the handle costs.
